## Duluth SEO company questions

### Do you work with businesses in Duluth and Gwinnett?

Yes. NativeCode is Atlanta-based, with its office in Norcross, next door to Duluth, and works with Duluth and Gwinnett businesses in person or remotely. Programs are scoped to the business, from a single storefront to several locations across the county.

### How should a bilingual site be set up for SEO?

Give each language its own URL, link the versions with hreflang tags and translate titles, descriptions and page content fully rather than mixing languages on one page. We build the structure, and translation is done by your team or a translator you trust.

### Does Google Business Profile support more than one language?

The profile has one primary language, but your description, posts and photos can reach both audiences, and reviews in either language count. Keeping your name, address and phone number the same everywhere matters more than the language of each individual listing.

### Can we rank in Suwanee or Lawrenceville from Duluth?

In regular search, yes, with a useful page for each area. Map results favor businesses close to the searcher, so service area businesses should list those cities as service areas. Each area page needs real local detail to rank, not a copy with the city name changed.

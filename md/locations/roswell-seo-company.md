### Do you work with businesses in Roswell?

### I run a home service business from my house. Can I still rank?

Yes. Set up Google Business Profile as a service area business, hide your home address and list the cities you serve. Then build a page for each main area and keep reviews coming. Distance still matters, so rankings are usually strongest closest to your base.

### How do restaurants on Canton Street get more visibility?

Keep the profile complete with current hours, menu, reservation link and fresh photos, respond to reviews and publish a fast site with the same details. Visitors often search on their phones nearby, so accurate details and strong photos help a restaurant stand out in map results.

### Are Local Services Ads worth it?

For many home service categories, yes. You pay per lead rather than per click, and the ads appear above the map results. We set them up alongside your organic profile, then compare cost per lead with other channels so budget goes where it works best.

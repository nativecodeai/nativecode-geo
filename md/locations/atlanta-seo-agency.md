## Atlanta SEO agency questions

### Is NativeCode based in Atlanta?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency with its office in Norcross, in the Atlanta metro. We work with Atlanta businesses in person or remotely, and with clients across the US. Local clients can meet the team that does the work rather than an account manager passing messages along.

### How do I rank in Google Maps in Atlanta?

Choose accurate categories, complete every profile field, earn steady reviews, keep your name, address and phone consistent and build a strong page for your location. Distance still matters, so win the area around your address first, then extend into nearby neighborhoods with service pages and reviews.

### Can you help us rank across the metro?

Yes. Service area pages for the towns you serve, local links and reviews from customers in those areas help you reach beyond your own neighborhood. Each page needs real local detail, so we start with the areas where you already have customers and work outward.

### What does local SEO cost in Atlanta?

It depends on the number of locations, the competition in your category and how much site and content work is needed. The best first step is the free SEO audit on this page, which shows where you stand today and what a program would need to cover.

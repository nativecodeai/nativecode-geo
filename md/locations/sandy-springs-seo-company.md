## Sandy Springs SEO company questions

### Do you work with businesses in Sandy Springs?

Yes. NativeCode is Atlanta-based, with its office in Norcross, and works with Sandy Springs businesses in person or remotely, from offices in Perimeter Center to local practices and services. Each program is sized to the business rather than a fixed package.

### How should a medical practice with several providers handle local SEO?

Give each location a complete Google Business Profile and a page on your site, add a page for each provider and keep details consistent across directories. Google's rules on practitioner profiles should be followed closely, so we set up profiles in line with those guidelines.

### Our office is in Perimeter Center. Should we target Dunwoody too?

If you serve clients there, yes. Perimeter Center spans both cities, and people search with either name. A page for each helps you appear for both, as long as each page explains what you offer to clients in that area and links back to your main location page.

### What do you report on?

We report calls, form leads, direction requests, bookings and AI mentions, by location, every month. Each report also lists the changes we made and what comes next, so you can connect the work to results instead of reading rankings on their own.

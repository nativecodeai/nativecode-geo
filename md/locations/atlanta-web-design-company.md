## Atlanta web design company questions

### Is NativeCode an Atlanta web design company?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based studio that designs and builds websites for businesses in the metro and across the US. We meet Atlanta clients in person or work remotely, whichever suits the project. The people who plan and design your site are the same people who build it, so nothing gets lost between teams.

### Which platforms do you build on?

We build on Webflow, Shopify, WordPress and custom code. Webflow suits marketing sites your team wants to edit. Shopify suits online stores. WordPress suits content-heavy sites already running on it. Custom code fits portals, tools and features no platform handles well. We recommend a platform once we understand your goals, team and budget.

### Will my new website rank in Google?

It can. We build search in from the start, though no one can honestly promise rankings. Every site ships with clean code, fast pages, schema markup and a page structure that matches how people search. Our founder has 15+ years in technical SEO, so search is planned from the first wireframe, not added after launch.

### How much does a website cost in Atlanta?

It depends on the number of pages, the platform, custom features and how much copy and content we need to create. A focused marketing site costs far less than a large store or a custom app. The best first step is the free website review on this page, which shows what your current site needs and what a rebuild would cover.

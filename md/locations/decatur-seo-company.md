## Decatur SEO company questions

### Do you work with small businesses in Decatur?

Yes. NativeCode is Atlanta-based, with its office in Norcross, and scopes local work to the size of the business, from a single storefront to several locations. Small businesses usually start with the Google Business Profile, reviews and one strong page before anything else is added.

### How can a local restaurant or shop beat chains in Google Maps?

Chains often have generic profiles. A local business with accurate categories, real photos, current hours, a menu or product list and frequent reviews can outrank them nearby. Replying to reviews and posting updates also shows Google and customers that the business is active.

### Should my site say Decatur or Atlanta?

Use Decatur if that is your address. Many people search Decatur by name, and Google uses your verified address for map results either way. If you also serve Atlanta neighborhoods, add pages for them rather than replacing Decatur, so you stay relevant for both.

### How many reviews do we need?

There is no fixed number. A steady flow of recent, detailed reviews matters more than a single burst, and responding to each one helps. We set up a simple way to ask customers after a visit or purchase, so reviews keep coming without extra work from your team.

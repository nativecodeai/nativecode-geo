## Alpharetta SEO company questions

### Do you work with tech companies in Alpharetta?

Yes. We run SEO, AI search and paid media for software and B2B companies, and we are Atlanta-based, with our office in Norcross, a short drive from Alpharetta. Most B2B programs focus on national search and AI answers, with local search kept tidy for branded and recruiting searches.

### Does a B2B company need local SEO?

Usually a little. A complete Google Business Profile and consistent company details help with branded and recruiting searches, but most B2B growth comes from national search and AI answers. We keep local work light for B2B firms and put the effort where buyers actually search.

### Can you help a restaurant or shop at Avalon or downtown?

Yes. For storefronts we focus on Google Business Profile, photos, menus or products, reviews and a fast site with current hours. Visitors to Avalon and downtown Alpharetta often search on their phones nearby, so map results and accurate details matter most for these businesses.

### Do you meet in person?

We can. NativeCode is Atlanta-based, with its office in Norcross, and works with North Fulton clients in person or remotely. Most projects run on shared reports and video calls, with in-person meetings for kickoffs, workshops or launches when they help the work move faster.

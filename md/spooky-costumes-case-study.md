# A Shopify store built for 8,000 costumes and one deadline.

NativeCode rebuilt and optimized a Shopify store selling Halloween costumes, masks, wigs and decor from brands like Fun World, Rubie's and Disguise, then connected it to its suppliers with EDI. Shoppers find the right costume, see real stock and get it before October 31.

## What did the store need?

The catalog is large and the selling window is short. Most demand lands in the weeks before Halloween, so the store has to help people decide quickly and make shipping times obvious.

02

## Catalog architecture

More than 8,000 products sit in 140 collections, organized by who is dressing, by character and theme, and by accessory. Each collection opens with a short buying guide and closes with an FAQ and related ideas, so it can rank on its own and send shoppers on to the next good option.

## Sizing at the point of sale

Costume sizes vary by brand, so every product page carries its own sizing help: how sizes work, what to check before ordering, and how to measure chest, waist, hips, height, inseam and head. Shipping and returns answers sit on the same page.

04

## How does supplier EDI work for the store?

Behind the storefront, we built EDI connections to the costume suppliers. Inventory flows into the store automatically and each order routes to the supplier that holds the stock, so shoppers see what is really available in peak season. Seasonal messaging on the front end, from the Halloween date to ship times and expedited checkout, tells them what will arrive in time.

FAQ

## Questions about working with NativeCode

### Are NativeCode jobs remote?

Yes. All current roles are remote within the US. Some roles, such as the Client Strategy Lead, need availability during US Eastern business hours for client meetings.

### Are these full-time jobs?

Current openings are fractional or contract roles for independent contractors. Scope, hours and rates are agreed with each person based on the work and their availability.

### How do I apply?

Open the role, read the full description and use the application form on that page. Include your LinkedIn profile or portfolio and a short note on your most relevant work.

### What happens after I apply?

Applications are reviewed against the role. If there is a fit, we set up a short video call, then agree on scope, availability and rates.

### What if none of the roles fit?

Email career@nativecode.ai with Careers in the subject line, a link to your work and a few lines on what you do best.

Security and trust

# Your systems stay yours.

NativeCode works with the least access an engagement needs. Client systems stay under client control unless access is expressly granted, and this page says plainly what we do, what we never touch, and what we are not certified for, so security and procurement teams can review us quickly.

[Contact security](mailto:security@nativecode.ai) [Request documentation](mailto:security@nativecode.ai?subject=Security%20Documentation%20Request)

At a glance

### No access by default

We do not need access to your systems to start. When access helps, you approve the scope and can revoke it at any time.

### Read-only first

Google Analytics and Search Console access, when granted, is normally read-only.

### MFA where supported

Multifactor authentication on company accounts and on client systems that support it.

### No payment data

We never need PCI or payment-card data, and we never move or commit client funds.

Our approach

## How does NativeCode protect client information?

Six principles cover how we handle access, devices and information across SEO, analytics, content, web and growth work.

01

### Limited access

Access to client systems is not required by default. When it is necessary, it is limited to approved systems and the minimum permissions required.

02

### Identity protection

Company accounts use strong passwords and multifactor authentication where supported.

03

### Secure devices

Client work happens on company-managed Apple devices with built-in macOS protections, encryption, screen locking and current updates.

04

### Data minimization

We collect and access only the information needed to perform the agreed services.

05

### Controlled sharing

Client information is not shared with unauthorized third parties or uploaded to external platforms or generative AI tools without authorization.

06

### Confidentiality

Client information is handled as confidential and used only for the purposes of the engagement.

Device security

## Managed machines, current software.

All client work is done on NativeCode-managed Apple devices used for business.

### Operating systems

Client work runs on supported versions of macOS.

### Built-in protections

XProtect, Gatekeeper, FileVault encryption, System Integrity Protection, the firewall and automatic security updates.

### Device access

Devices are protected by Touch ID or strong device authentication.

### Screen locking

Screens lock automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity or less.

### Software updates

System and app updates are installed promptly. Critical security updates are targeted within seven days, sooner when severity calls for it.

### Business use

Client work is done only on NativeCode-managed devices used for business purposes.

FAQ

## Common security questions

### Does NativeCode require access to client systems?

No. NativeCode does not require access to client systems by default, and most SEO, content and strategy work can start without it. If access becomes necessary for an engagement, the client must expressly approve it, the scope is limited to what the work needs, and the client can revoke it at any time.

### What type of system access does NativeCode typically use?

Read-only access is preferred whenever possible. For analytics and search-performance platforms such as Google Analytics and Search Console, NativeCode normally asks for read-only access. Edit access is requested only when an engagement requires changes inside a platform, and only with the express approval of the client.

### Does NativeCode access payment-card data?

No. NativeCode does not require access to PCI or payment-card data for its standard services, including SEO, analytics, content, web and growth work. We also never move or commit client funds. Payment systems and card data stay entirely under the control of the client and its payment providers.

### Does NativeCode use multifactor authentication?

Yes. NativeCode uses multifactor authentication on supported company accounts and on client systems that support it. MFA protects the accounts used for analytics, search, content and web work, so a stolen password alone is not enough to sign in. Where a platform does not support MFA, access is kept as limited as possible.

### Is NativeCode SOC 2 certified?

No. NativeCode is not currently SOC 2 certified, and we say so plainly so procurement teams can plan around it. We follow documented security practices covering access, devices, data handling and incident response, and we answer security questionnaires and vendor-risk reviews on request through security@nativecode.ai.

### Is NativeCode ISO 27001 certified?

No. NativeCode is not currently ISO 27001 certified. Our security practices are written down instead, with policies covering information security, access control, passwords and MFA, acceptable use, data handling and incident response. Security teams can request this documentation or send a questionnaire to security@nativecode.ai.

### Does NativeCode upload client information to generative AI systems?

No, not without client authorization. NativeCode does not upload confidential client information to external generative AI systems unless the client has approved it. This covers documents, data exports, analytics and any other material shared during an engagement, and it applies to every engagement we run.

### Can NativeCode comply with client VPN or trusted-device requirements?

Yes, when required and technically appropriate for the engagement. NativeCode can work through client VPNs, on trusted or managed devices, and within other access controls a client security team sets. Share your requirements during scoping so access is set up correctly before any work begins.

### How can a security team request more information?

Email security@nativecode.ai. We respond to reasonable security, procurement and vendor-risk questions, complete security questionnaires and share our written security documentation on request. Include your company name, the engagement in question and any deadline, so we can reply with what your review needs.

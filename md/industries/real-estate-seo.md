## Real estate SEO questions

### Can a local brokerage compete with national portals?

On local and neighborhood searches, yes. Portals win broad listing searches, but detailed neighborhood guides, market updates and strong Google Business Profiles let local firms win the questions buyers ask before they pick an area or agent. Those searches are where local knowledge shows.

### Do IDX listings help SEO?

They can, if listing pages stay crawlable on your domain and add something portals do not have, such as agent notes or neighborhood context. Plain IDX feeds repeat content found on many other sites, so they rarely rank on their own without that added local detail.

### What should a neighborhood page include?

A neighborhood page should describe what it is like to live there: housing types and price ranges, commute options, parks and amenities, and recent market activity. It should be written from local knowledge, link to current listings and be updated regularly so the details stay accurate.

### Does this work for property managers and builders?

Yes. Communities and developments need a page for each property with floor plans, amenities and availability, plus local visibility for the searches renters and buyers use. Each property also benefits from its own Google Business Profile where it has a leasing or sales office.

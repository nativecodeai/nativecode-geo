FAQ

## Nonprofit SEO questions

### What is Google Ad Grants?

Google Ad Grants is a Google program that gives eligible nonprofits free search advertising credit each month. Eligibility rules and account requirements are set by Google and listed on the Google for Nonprofits site. We can set up and manage the account so it stays compliant with those rules.

### Is SEO worth it for a small nonprofit?

Yes, when it is focused. A few strong pages on your programs and the issues you work on, plus a complete Google Business Profile, often bring more support than a broad campaign. Small teams get the most from fixing what exists before publishing anything new.

### How can we raise more recurring donations?

Make monthly giving the clear default on a fast, on-site form, show what each amount does and follow up with stewardship email that reports impact. Donors who see results are more likely to keep giving, so the email after the gift matters as much as the form.

### Can you report results to our board?

Yes. We report donations, sign-ups and inquiries from search, ads and email in plain terms, with the cost behind each. Reports are short enough to share in a board pack, with notes on what changed and what we plan next, so trustees can follow the work.

FAQ

## Education SEO questions

### How can a school outrank directory and lead-gen sites?

By publishing program pages that answer more than the directories do: cost, schedule, outcomes and admission steps, with clear structure and schema. Directories rank on breadth. Schools win on depth for their own programs, because they hold the details no third party can match.

### Should each program have its own page?

Yes. One page per program, with its own title, details and FAQ, lets each one rank for its own searches and gives ads a focused landing page. A single page listing every program rarely ranks for any of them and makes it harder for students to compare options.

### Does SEO help online course providers?

Yes. Topic guides, course pages with Course schema and comparison content reach learners while they are still deciding, which lowers the cost of each enrollment over time. Organic traffic keeps arriving after ad budgets pause, so it steadies enrollment between campaigns.

### How long is a typical enrollment funnel?

It varies by program, from weeks for short courses to many months for degrees. That is why we pair search with email nurture, so interest is not lost between the first visit and the application. Each stage gets content that answers the questions students ask at that point.

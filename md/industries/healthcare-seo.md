## Healthcare SEO questions

### What is YMYL and why does it matter for healthcare SEO?

YMYL stands for Your Money or Your Life, Google's term for topics that can affect a person's health, finances or safety. Health pages are held to higher standards of expertise and trust, so authorship, medical review and sourcing matter more than usual, and thin or unsourced pages struggle to rank.

### Do health pages need a medical reviewer?

For pages that give medical information, a named, qualified reviewer with a visible review date is one of the clearest trust signals. We set up the review workflow, the author and reviewer pages and the page elements that show it, so every update records who checked the content and when.

### Can telehealth companies rank in multiple states?

Yes, with a page for each state where the service is licensed, covering what is offered there and how care works. Pages for states you cannot serve should not exist. Each state page links to the relevant services and providers, so search engines can see the coverage is real.

### Is it safe to use ad pixels on a healthcare site?

It needs care. Standard pixels can send page and form data to ad platforms, which is a privacy risk on health pages. We set up tracking with your compliance team so measurement does not expose patient information, using server-side options and limited events where they fit.

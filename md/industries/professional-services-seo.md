FAQ

## Professional services SEO questions

### Does SEO work for firms that win on referrals?

Yes. Referred clients still search your firm and your people before they call, and search brings in work beyond your network. A strong site supports both. Clear service pages and expert profiles make it easy for a referral to confirm they found the right firm.

### Who writes the content if our partners are busy?

We do. A short interview gives us enough to draft an article in the partner's voice, and they review it before it is published. This keeps the expertise real and the time cost low, usually a short call and one round of edits per article.

### Should we have pages for each industry we serve?

Yes, where you have real experience in that industry. Sector pages rank for searches like an accountant for restaurants and show buyers you understand their business. Pages for sectors you have never served should not exist, because buyers and AI tools can tell.

### How do we measure results?

By qualified inquiries and consultations booked from search and AI referrals, tracked in analytics and your CRM, not by traffic alone. We report which pages and topics bring the inquiries, so content time goes to the subjects that actually win work.

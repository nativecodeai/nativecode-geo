## Home services SEO questions

### How do home service companies get into the Google map results?

Google says local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence. A complete Google Business Profile with the right categories and services, steady reviews, consistent citations and strong service pages all help. Distance from the searcher still matters, so start with the area around your base.

### Should we make a page for every town we serve?

Only if each page has real local content, such as jobs done there and details specific to the area. Copies with the town name swapped can be treated as doorway pages and hurt the whole site. A few strong area pages usually beat many thin ones.

### How many reviews do we need?

There is no fixed number. What matters is a steady flow of recent, detailed reviews compared with competitors near you. We set up a simple process to ask after every job, and we help you reply to each review, which also shows future customers how you treat people.

### Do service area businesses need to show an address on Google?

No. Businesses that go to their customers can hide the address and set a service area instead, as Google's guidelines allow. The profile still needs a verified address behind the scenes, and the service area should list only the places you actually serve.

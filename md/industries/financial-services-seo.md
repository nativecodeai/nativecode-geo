FAQ

## Financial services SEO questions

### How do you handle compliance review?

We plan content with your compliance team from the start. Sources, claims and disclosures are agreed in the brief, so review moves faster and fewer pages are rewritten after drafting. Each page keeps a record of what was approved and when, which makes later updates easier to clear.

### Do calculators help financial SEO?

Often, yes. A well-built calculator answers a specific question, keeps visitors on the page and earns links. We build them so the math and assumptions are shown on the page, which helps compliance review and gives AI tools a clear, quotable explanation of how the result was reached.

### How do you keep rates current across the site?

We set up one source for rates, fees and effective dates that feeds every page using them. A change is made once, and the date shown stays accurate everywhere. This prevents old numbers lingering on forgotten pages, where search engines and AI tools could still find them.

### Can a smaller firm compete with comparison sites?

Yes, on the right searches. Aggregators win broad product terms, but specific, local and expert questions are often answered better by a provider that explains its own products clearly. We focus on those searches first, where a smaller firm has the advantage of direct knowledge.

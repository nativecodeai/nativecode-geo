FAQ

## Manufacturing SEO questions

### Does SEO work for niche industrial products?

Yes. Search volume is lower, but the people searching are often engineers or buyers with a real need. Ranking for specific parts, specs and applications brings fewer visits and more qualified ones, which suits sales teams that measure success in quotes rather than traffic.

### Should we turn our PDF catalog into web pages?

For your main products, yes. HTML pages with specs and downloads rank better, are easier to compare and can carry Product schema. Keep the PDFs as downloads on those pages, so buyers who need the full document can still get it in one click.

### Can you connect the website to our ERP or EDI?

Yes. We connect ecommerce and B2B portals to ERP systems and EDI trading partners so orders, inventory and invoices move without manual entry. That keeps stock levels and lead times on the website accurate, which matters a great deal to buyers comparing several suppliers.

### How do you write technical content without getting it wrong?

We interview your engineers and product managers, draft from their input and have them review every page before it goes live. Specs come from your own data sheets, and any number on a page is checked against the source so buyers and AI tools get the right figure.

The search problem

## Why do large catalogs struggle in search?

Large catalogs struggle because every new product, filter and collection creates URLs. Without a plan, most of them compete with each other or never get indexed at all.

01

### Index bloat

Filters, sorting and variants can turn one category into thousands of near-identical URLs that waste crawl budget and split rankings.

02

### Thin category pages

Collection pages are where most commercial searches land, yet many are only a product grid with nothing that helps a shopper choose.

03

### Product data AI cannot read

AI shopping answers lean on structured product facts. Missing price, availability, sizing or review markup keeps products out of them.

FAQ

## Ecommerce SEO questions

### How is ecommerce SEO different from regular SEO?

Ecommerce SEO is mostly architecture. The work centers on which category, filter and product URLs should exist and be indexed, and on making those pages useful to shoppers. Blog content plays a supporting role. Product schema, feeds and internal links between collections matter more on a store than on most other sites.

### Do you work on Shopify?

Yes. Most of our ecommerce work is on Shopify, including custom themes, collection structure, product schema and large catalog builds. We also work on other platforms, and we plan migrations to Shopify with full URL mapping and redirects so existing rankings carry over to the new store.

### Should filter pages be indexed?

Only the filter combinations people actually search for should be indexed, such as a color or material within a category. The rest stay usable for shoppers but are kept out of the index so they do not compete with core collection pages or waste crawl budget on near-duplicate URLs.

### How do products get into AI shopping answers?

AI tools draw on product data from the page, structured markup and shopping feeds, plus reviews and mentions elsewhere. Accurate, consistent product facts and clear buying guides make a store easier to cite. We check how ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews describe your products and fix the gaps.

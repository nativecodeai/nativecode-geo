## Why do booking sites outrank hotels and restaurants?

Booking sites outrank hotels and restaurants because online travel agencies and review platforms rank for most travel searches, then take a commission on the bookings they send.

## Hospitality SEO questions

### How can a hotel get more direct bookings from search?

By ranking its own pages for the searches guests use, such as the hotel name, the area and nearby attractions, and by making booking on the site easy. A complete Google Business Profile that links to your booking engine also helps guests book you without going through a third party.

### Do restaurants need SEO beyond Google Maps?

Maps matter most, but a fast site with the current menu, hours and reservation link supports the profile and answers the questions people ask before they choose. Keeping those details the same on the site, the profile and reservation platforms avoids confusing both guests and search engines.

### What schema should a hotel use?

A hotel should use Hotel or LodgingBusiness markup with address, amenities and contact details, plus review and FAQ markup where the page shows that content. Markup should always match what is on the page, and it should be checked after each site update so it stays valid.

### Can vacation rentals rank without the big platforms?

Yes, for searches about the property name, the area and specific stays. A direct site with detailed pages, local guides and reviews can win those searches and reduce reliance on platform fees. Most owners keep platform listings too and use the site to capture repeat and referral guests.

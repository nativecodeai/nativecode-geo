FAQ

## Law firm SEO questions

### Can a law firm outrank legal directories?

For many searches, yes. Directories win broad terms, but firms with strong practice area pages, a complete Google Business Profile and consistent local citations can win the map results and specific questions. Those specific searches often come from people who are closer to hiring a lawyer.

### Do attorney advertising rules affect SEO?

Yes. State bar rules can limit claims about results, specialization and testimonials. We write within the rules for each state you practice in and flag anything that needs your review before it goes live. Reviews and case descriptions are handled with the same care.

### Should each practice area have its own page?

Yes. Each practice area answers different questions and is searched separately, so one page per practice area works better than a single services page. Each page should explain the process, common questions and who handles the work, with links to the right attorney profiles.

### How do people find lawyers through AI tools?

They ask what to do after an incident or who handles a certain type of case nearby. Answers draw on firm websites, directories and reviews, so clear practice pages and consistent listings matter. We check how AI tools describe your firm and correct the sources behind wrong answers.

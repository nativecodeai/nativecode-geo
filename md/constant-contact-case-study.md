# An SEO, AEO and GEO program for Constant Contact.

NativeCode, an Atlanta-based agency, runs an ongoing SEO, AEO and GEO program with Constant Contact's growth marketing, SEO and content teams. Every page is built to rank in Google, get cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity, and lead the reader to a clear next step.

## What did Constant Contact need?

Constant Contact wanted search visibility that does more than drive traffic. Every page had to answer a real question, earn citations in AI answers from Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity, and point the reader to one destination, such as a partner signup or a connected integration.

02

## How is the program structured?

The work runs in tracks, each built around one audience and one destination. Anchor guides hold the main question in a search group, and supporting pieces each answer one question completely and link back. The same audiences are covered on the blog and in the Constant Contact Community, written in the peer-to-peer voice of the boards.

## How is each page built to be quoted?

A page can rank and still never be cited. Every piece opens with a complete answer in forty to sixty words, uses the questions people actually type as headings, sets comparisons in tables, names products in full and dates its claims, so answer engines can lift it and verify it.

04

## Growth programs

The engagement has grown into an organic discoverability program around the Canva and Constant Contact integration, covering search, AI answers, content and destination pages, with monthly reporting on rankings, share of answer and conversion.

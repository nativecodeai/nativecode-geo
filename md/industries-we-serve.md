## Industries we serve: common questions

### Which industries does NativeCode work with?

NativeCode works with ecommerce, SaaS, healthcare, financial services, legal, real estate, home services, hospitality, education, manufacturing, professional services and nonprofit organizations. Each has its own page explaining the search problems we solve there. We also take on businesses outside these twelve when the work fits.

### Do you specialize by industry?

Yes and no. Each industry page reflects a different search problem, such as compliance in finance, map results in home services or catalog structure in ecommerce. The team and methods stay the same, so every client gets SEO, AI search, websites and ads planned together.

### Do you work with businesses outside Atlanta?

Yes. NativeCode is Atlanta-based, with its office in Norcross, and works with clients across the US. Most programs run remotely on shared reports and regular calls, so industry fit matters more than location when deciding whether we are the right team.

### What if my industry is not listed?

Tell us about your market. We work beyond these twelve industries, and the same approach applies: find how your customers search and decide, then build the pages, profiles and campaigns that reach them. The free audit is a good way to see whether we can help.

# Product pages that answer the fit question before checkout.

NativeCode rebuilt HauteFlair's bra product pages around the question that decides the sale: will it fit? A size calculator, fit guidance, reviews beside the size selector and FAQ blocks now sit where the decision happens, with product schema that helps the pages rank.

## What was stopping shoppers from buying?

Bra shoppers buy on fit. A size chart in a separate tab leaves the hardest question unanswered at the moment of decision, and a shopper who is unsure of their size leaves to find out somewhere else.

02

## What changed on the product pages?

We put a bra size calculator and fit guidance directly on the product page, moved reviews up beside the size selector, and added FAQ blocks that answer sizing and returns questions in place. Collection page copy was rewritten so shoppers land on the right product to begin with.

## Built for search too

The new blocks are real page content, not pop-ups, so they help the page rank as well as convert. Product schema describes the item, and the FAQ answers are written to be quoted in search results and AI answers.

04

## The result

Conversion rate rose on the rebuilt product pages. The work also fed the size and fit thinking behind the sizing products we build today.

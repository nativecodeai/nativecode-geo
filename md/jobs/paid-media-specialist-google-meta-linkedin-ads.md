## About the role

We are looking for a hands-on paid media specialist to build and run campaigns for our clients. You will own day-to-day account work on Google Ads, Meta Ads and LinkedIn Ads, keep tracking accurate, and turn results into reporting our client team can present with confidence.

You will manage accounts with real budgets and clear goals, usually cost per lead, cost per sale or return on ad spend. We set up tracking before spend starts, so you can trust the numbers you optimize against.

## Who you will work with

You will work with the client strategy lead who presents results, our analytics and tracking team, and our creative team for new ads and landing pages.

## What you will do

- Build and launch search, Performance Max, Shopping, Meta and LinkedIn campaigns.
- Manage budgets, bids, audiences, keywords and negative lists across accounts.
- Set up and check conversion tracking with Google Tag Manager, GA4 and platform pixels.
- Test ad copy, creative and landing pages, and act on what the data shows.
- Prepare weekly and monthly performance reports with clear next steps.
- Flag problems early and recommend fixes to the client strategy lead.

## What success looks like in your first 90 days

- You have audited each assigned account and shared a prioritized fix list.
- Conversion tracking is verified end to end in every account you run.
- Weekly optimization notes and monthly reports go out on schedule.
- At least one account shows measurable improvement in cost per lead or return on ad spend.

## What we are looking for

- 3+ years managing Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts, ideally at an agency.
- Hands-on LinkedIn Ads experience for B2B lead generation.
- Comfort with GA4, Google Tag Manager and conversion tracking.
- A record of improving cost per lead or return on ad spend.
- Clear written communication and organized reporting.

## Nice to have

- Google Ads certification.
- Experience with Microsoft Ads, TikTok Ads or Google Merchant Center.
- Familiarity with offline conversion imports from HubSpot or Salesforce.

## Tools you will use

Google Ads, Google Merchant Center, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, Looker Studio and Microsoft Ads.

## Compensation and working arrangement

Ongoing contract work as an independent contractor, paid by project or retainer. Hours are flexible, with some overlap with US Eastern time for client deadlines. Workload grows with client demand.

Rates are agreed before work starts, based on experience and the accounts you manage.

# Client Strategy Lead, Digital Marketing (Fractional)

Lead communication with NativeCode clients, present results and strategy across SEO, content marketing and paid media, and keep client relationships strong.

Remote, US

Hours

Meetings during US Eastern business hours

Type

Independent contractor

## About the role

We are looking for an experienced marketing professional to lead communication with existing NativeCode clients. You will represent NativeCode in meetings, present performance results and project updates, discuss strategy, and build strong relationships across SEO, content marketing, and paid media engagements.

Our implementation team prepares reports and deliverables and handles day-to-day execution. You will review the work before meetings, understand the recommendations, and lead productive client discussions.

You should be comfortable working with marketing leaders at B2B, SaaS, and ecommerce companies. You’ll help clients understand progress, priorities, and next steps while ensuring their feedback reaches the right people on our team.

## Who you will work with

You will work with our leadership team on account strategy, our implementation team on delivery, and each client’s marketing lead, growth team, or other key stakeholders.

## What you will do

- Serve as a primary point of contact for assigned clients and lead regular check-ins and strategy reviews.
- Present performance reports, content briefs, editorial plans, SEO updates, and paid media recommendations prepared by our team.
- Explain results and recommendations clearly, connecting the work to each client’s business goals.
- Answer questions within your expertise and involve our specialists when deeper input is needed.
- Review materials before meetings and coordinate with our team on findings, recommendations, and outstanding questions.
- Translate client feedback into clear priorities, action items, and next steps.
- Track commitments and coordinate follow-ups to keep projects moving.
- Identify opportunities to improve results or provide additional support.
- Contribute to execution where project needs and your expertise align.

## What success looks like in your first 90 days

- You understand your assigned clients’ businesses, goals, scopes, and current priorities.
- You lead meetings confidently, with clear preparation, recommendations, and agreed next steps.
- Client feedback and action items reach the implementation team promptly, and commitments are tracked through completion.
- You keep clients informed and raise potential issues early.
- You contribute practical recommendations that strengthen results and client relationships.

## What we are looking for

- 5+ years in a client-facing marketing role at an agency or consultancy.
- A strong understanding of SEO and content marketing, with working knowledge of Google Ads, Meta Ads, and LinkedIn Ads.
- Experience working with B2B or SaaS clients.
- Ability to explain performance metrics and recommendations clearly to business stakeholders.
- Confidence leading meetings, responding thoughtfully to questions, and coordinating specialist input.
- Strong preparation, organization, and follow-through.
- Ability to learn each client’s business and represent NativeCode professionally.

## Nice to have

- Hands-on experience in SEO, content strategy, or paid media.
- Familiarity with GA4, Google Search Console, Looker Studio, and CRM reporting.
- Experience with AI search visibility, including Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity.
- Experience supporting account renewals or expanding existing client engagements.

## Tools and platforms

Depending on the account, you may review reports or work in Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Looker Studio, Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, and client CRM systems.

## Compensation and working arrangement

This is an ongoing fractional engagement as an independent contractor, with client meetings during US Eastern business hours.

Scope and compensation are agreed before work begins, based on assigned accounts, meeting frequency, preparation, and follow-up responsibilities. Compensation may be structured as a monthly retainer or hourly rate.

Execution responsibilities, where applicable, are agreed upon by project.

## How we hire

1. Apply using the form on this page, including your résumé, LinkedIn profile, availability, and preferred rate.
2. We review your experience against the role.
3. If there is a fit, we schedule a 30-minute video call.
4. A second conversation explores your past work and how you would approach client meetings and account scenarios.
5. We agree on scope, availability, and compensation, then sign a contractor agreement.

NativeCode is an Atlanta-based digital marketing agency providing SEO and AI search, content marketing, web design and development, paid media, email marketing, and creative services to companies across the US, including B2B and SaaS brands.

You’ll work closely with our leadership team and a remote team of marketing specialists.

NativeCode welcomes applicants of every background. We evaluate candidates based on relevant skills, experience, and ability to succeed in the role.

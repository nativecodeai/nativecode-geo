## About the role

We are looking for an email marketing specialist who builds directly in client accounts. Most of the work is in Klaviyo, with some Mailchimp and Constant Contact. You will set up flows, segments and campaigns that are measured on revenue, not opens.

Many of our ecommerce clients run on Shopify and Klaviyo, while B2B clients often use Mailchimp, Constant Contact or HubSpot. You will log in to client accounts and build the work directly, not hand over documents for someone else to set up.

## Who you will work with

You will work with our founder and client strategy lead on goals, our designers on email templates, and the client's ecommerce or marketing team.

## What you will do

- Build and improve automated flows: welcome, browse and cart abandonment, post-purchase and win-back.
- Set up segmentation, list growth forms and SMS in Klaviyo.
- Plan and send campaigns with clear goals and tests.
- Monitor deliverability, authentication and list health.
- Report on flow and campaign revenue and recommend next steps.

## What success looks like in your first 90 days

- Core flows are live or rebuilt for each assigned account: welcome, abandonment, post-purchase and win-back.
- Domain authentication and list hygiene are checked and fixed where needed.
- A campaign calendar is in place and campaigns go out on schedule.
- Flow and campaign revenue is reported monthly with clear next steps.

## What we are looking for

- 3+ years hands-on in Klaviyo, ideally for Shopify brands.
- Working experience in Mailchimp or Constant Contact.
- Understanding of email deliverability, DNS authentication and list hygiene.
- Comfort with segmentation logic and Klaviyo's reporting.
- Good copy instincts and attention to detail.

## Nice to have

- Klaviyo certification.
- HTML email or template-building skills.
- B2B lifecycle email experience in HubSpot.

## Tools you will use

Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Constant Contact, HubSpot, Shopify, Figma for email design review, and Google Analytics 4.

## Compensation and working arrangement

Ongoing contract work as an independent contractor, paid by project or retainer, with flexible hours.

Rates are agreed before work starts, based on experience and scope.

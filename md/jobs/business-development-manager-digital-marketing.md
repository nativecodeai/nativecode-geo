# Business Development Manager, Digital Marketing (Fractional)

Find and win new clients for NativeCode's SEO, paid media and digital growth services, with a focus on B2B and SaaS companies.

Engagement

Fractional

Location

Remote, US

Hours

US business hours

Type

Independent contractor

## About the role

We are looking for a business development professional to connect NativeCode with companies that need SEO, paid media and digital growth support. You will find potential clients, build relationships with decision-makers and help turn qualified opportunities into new business. You will work directly with our leadership and delivery team to match each prospect with the right services.

NativeCode sells SEO, AI search, paid media, web design and development, and email marketing to companies across the US. You will open doors with B2B and SaaS teams, qualify what they need, and work with our team to turn the right opportunities into signed engagements.

## Who you will work with

You will work with our leadership team on pricing and proposals, and with our delivery leads, who join sales calls when a prospect needs technical depth.

## What you will do

- Identify and pursue potential clients, with a focus on B2B and SaaS companies.
- Generate opportunities through targeted outreach, networking, referrals and partnerships.
- Connect with marketing leaders, founders and other decision-makers.
- Lead introductory calls to understand each prospect's needs, budget and timeline.
- Work with our team to shape proposals and present our services.
- Manage follow-ups and move opportunities through to signed agreements.
- Keep an accurate pipeline and share regular progress updates.

## What success looks like in your first 90 days

- You have built a target account list and outreach plan for B2B and SaaS companies.
- You are booking a steady flow of qualified introductory calls each month.
- Several proposals are out with prospects you sourced, and at least one is moving toward a signed agreement.
- Your pipeline in the CRM is accurate and reviewed weekly.

## What we are looking for

- A track record of winning new business for a digital marketing agency or consultancy.
- Working knowledge of SEO, paid media and digital marketing services.
- History of generating qualified opportunities and closing service engagements.
- Strong outreach, relationship-building and communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and follow through consistently.
- Experience selling to B2B or SaaS companies.

## Nice to have

- An existing network of marketing leaders or founders.
- Experience with a CRM such as HubSpot, Pipedrive or Salesforce.

## Tools you will use

HubSpot or a similar CRM, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Google Workspace, Zoom or Google Meet, and Slack.

## Compensation and working arrangement

Ongoing fractional engagement as an independent contractor, with availability during US business hours. Scope, targets and compensation are agreed based on experience and responsibilities. Our team supports you with service details, work examples, proposal development and technical expertise on sales calls.

Structured as a base retainer, commission on closed business, or both, agreed up front based on experience and targets.

## How we hire

1. Apply with the form on this page.
2. We review your application against the role.
3. If there is a fit, we set up a 30-minute video call.
4. A second conversation walks through your past work and how you would approach our clients.
5. We agree on scope, availability and rate, then sign a contractor agreement.

## About NativeCode

NativeCode is an Atlanta-based digital marketing agency. We run SEO and AI search, web design and development, paid media, email marketing and creative for companies across the US, including B2B and SaaS brands. The team is senior, small and remote-first.

NativeCode welcomes applicants of every background. We make decisions based on skills, experience and fit for the work.

## About the role

We are looking for a technical SEO specialist who goes deep on how sites are crawled, indexed and understood by search engines and AI answer engines. You will turn crawl data, logs and Search Console findings into fixes that ship.

Our SEO work focuses on site architecture, structured data and visibility in AI answers from Google, ChatGPT and Perplexity, not just keyword rankings. Clients range from large ecommerce catalogs to B2B and SaaS sites on Shopify, Webflow and WordPress.

## Who you will work with

You will work with our founder on strategy, our Webflow and Shopify developers who ship your fixes, and the client's marketing and engineering teams.

## What you will do

- Run technical audits covering crawl, indexation, rendering, speed and site structure.
- Plan site architecture, internal linking and URL changes, including migrations and redirects.
- Write and validate JSON-LD structured data across templates.
- Track how Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity see and cite client sites.
- Turn findings into clear tickets for developers and check the fixes.

## What success looks like in your first 90 days

- You have completed technical audits for assigned sites with prioritized, developer-ready tickets.
- Structured data is validated across key templates.
- Crawl and indexation issues are tracked and closing on schedule.
- AI search visibility is being measured for each client.

## What we are looking for

- 4+ years of hands-on technical SEO.
- Experience with Screaming Frog or Sitebulb, Search Console and log analysis.
- Working knowledge of schema.org and structured data testing.
- Experience on Shopify, Webflow or WordPress sites.
- Precise, data-backed reasoning and clear writing.

## Nice to have

- Python or similar for audit automation.
- Large ecommerce catalog or enterprise migration experience.

## Tools you will use

Screaming Frog or Sitebulb, Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Ahrefs or Semrush, Schema.org validators, Chrome DevTools and log file tools.

## Compensation and working arrangement

Ongoing contract work as an independent contractor, paid by project or retainer, with flexible hours.

Rates are agreed before work starts, based on experience and scope.

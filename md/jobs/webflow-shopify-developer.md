## About the role

We are looking for a developer who builds Webflow and Shopify sites that are fast, clean and easy for clients to run. You will work from our designs and SEO plans and own the build from first template to launch.

Every site we build is planned around search from the start: clean CMS structures, fast templates, valid schema and safe redirects. You will turn designs and SEO plans into production sites that clients can edit without breaking.

## Who you will work with

You will work with our designers, our technical SEO specialist and our founder, who reviews builds before launch.

## What you will do

- Build Webflow sites with clean CMS structures, components and semantic markup.
- Develop and customize Shopify themes, sections and collection structures.
- Add JSON-LD structured data to templates and validate it.
- Handle migrations with URL mapping and 301 redirects so rankings carry over.
- Tune Core Web Vitals and fix crawl and indexation issues at build time.

## What success looks like in your first 90 days

- You have shipped or improved at least one Webflow or Shopify build to production.
- Templates pass Core Web Vitals checks and schema validation.
- Every launch includes a redirect map and post-launch checks.
- Handoff notes are clear enough for the client team to manage content.

## What we are looking for

- 3+ years building production Webflow sites.
- Shopify theme development experience with Liquid.
- Strong HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
- Understanding of why site structure matters for search.
- Clean, maintainable builds and clear handoff notes.

## Nice to have

- Shopify Plus, Checkout Extensibility or app integration experience.
- WordPress or headless builds.

## Tools you will use

Webflow, Shopify (Liquid, Online Store 2.0, Shopify CLI), GitHub, Figma, Chrome DevTools and PageSpeed Insights.

## Compensation and working arrangement

Ongoing contract work as an independent contractor, paid by project, with flexible hours.

Rates are agreed before work starts, based on experience and scope.

## How we hire

1. Apply with the form on this page.

3. If there is a fit, we set up a 30-minute video call.

5. We agree on scope, availability and rate, then sign a contractor agreement.

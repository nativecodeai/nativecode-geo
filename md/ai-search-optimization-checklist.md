## AI search optimization questions

### What is AI search optimization?

AI search optimization is the work of making your site easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI Overviews to find, understand and cite. It is often called AEO (answer engine optimization) or GEO (generative engine optimization), and it covers crawler access, structured data, entity clarity and answer-ready content.

### Is it different from SEO?

AI search optimization builds on SEO rather than replacing it. Crawlability, structured data and authority still matter. What changes is that AI answers are assembled from individual sections of pages, so content has to be clear, specific and quotable on its own, and your brand has to be described the same way across the web.

### Should we block AI crawlers?

Usually not, and never by accident. Treat search crawlers and training crawlers separately. Blocking search crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot or PerplexityBot keeps you out of those answers. Blocking training crawlers such as GPTBot is a business decision that does not affect Google AI Overviews, so check your robots.txt and CDN rules first.

### How do we measure visibility in AI answers?

Measure it with a mix of traffic data and repeat testing. Track AI referral traffic in GA4, test a fixed set of buyer questions in each assistant every month, log when you are named or cited, and add an AI option to the how did you hear about us field on your forms.

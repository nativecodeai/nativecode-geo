# A trust-first store for a sensitive category.

NativeCode rebuilt a Shopify store for a sexual wellness retailer selling brands like We-Vibe, Womanizer, LELO and Lovense. In this category privacy decides whether people buy, so the store was rebuilt around discretion and clear answers, and connected to its suppliers with EDI.

## What did shoppers need from the store?

Shoppers in this category worry about what arrives at the door and what shows on a statement. The store had to answer those worries up front, then make a very large catalog easy to browse.

02

## Catalog architecture

More than 11,000 products are organized into 90 collections by type, with a mega menu that goes straight to specific product types. Each collection opens with a plain-language introduction, a product count and subtype shortcuts, then closes with a buying guide and common questions written to rank and to be quoted in AI answers.

## How does the store protect shopper privacy?

Every order ships in a plain, unbranded box with a neutral billing name, and the store says so in the header, on collection pages and on every product page. Shipping, returns and care questions are answered right beside the buy button.

04

## Supplier connections

The catalog draws on several wholesale and dropship suppliers. We built EDI connections to them, so inventory syncs into the store and each order routes to the supplier that holds the stock. That is what keeps more than 11,000 products accurate without manual updates.

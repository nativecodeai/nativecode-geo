## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Wilmington?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Wilmington businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Wilmington work. We do not have a Wilmington office.

### Can I rank for Philadelphia searches from Wilmington?

Maps favors nearby businesses, so searches in Philadelphia itself are hard to win from Wilmington. You can still appear for searches near the state line. Pages about the Pennsylvania areas you serve can help in organic results. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### Does Delaware's lack of sales tax matter for local SEO?

It can help retailers. Shoppers from Pennsylvania and New Jersey look for Delaware stores to save on tax. Make your address, hours and parking clear so those visitors can plan a trip. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

### Should I mention Brandywine or Pike Creek on my site?

Yes, if you serve those areas. Many people search by area name in northern New Castle County. Add real details about your work there instead of repeating your Wilmington page. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

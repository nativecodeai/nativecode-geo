## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Maryland?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Maryland. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Maryland work. We do not have an office in Maryland.

### I'm in Columbia. Should I target Baltimore or D.C. searches?

Start with where your customers actually live, usually Howard County and nearby areas. Pages for the cities you serve help, but map results favor close businesses, so wide rankings are not promised. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### Do I need separate pages for Baltimore City and Baltimore County?

Often yes. Baltimore City is separate from Baltimore County, and county residents in Towson or Catonsville may search by their own area. Clear pages help Google and AI tools match you. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

### How do AI assistants pick Maryland businesses to mention?

They draw on Google Business Profile data, reviews, directories and your website. Consistent name, address and phone details plus clear service area pages give them reliable facts. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in New Orleans?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with New Orleans businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real New Orleans work. We do not have a New Orleans office.

### How do I reach tourists searching in the French Quarter?

Keep hours, menus and photos current, reply to reviews and add attributes visitors filter by. Tourists search on phones near their hotel, so proximity and accurate details matter most. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

### Should I target Metairie separately from New Orleans?

Yes, if you serve it. Metairie is in Jefferson Parish, and residents often search with that name. A page with real service details for Metairie helps you match those searches. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### How should I prepare my listing for Mardi Gras?

Update special hours early, note road closures or parade-day changes and post about any events. Accurate details help Maps and AI tools give visitors the right answers. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Miami?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Miami businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Miami work. We do not have a Miami office.

### Should I build Spanish pages for Miami customers?

For most Miami businesses, yes. Spanish is widely spoken across Miami-Dade. Have a fluent speaker write the pages, list Spanish on your profile and answer Spanish reviews in Spanish. Keep your business name, address and phone number identical in every language version so search engines treat them as one business.

### Can a Doral or Kendall business show up for Miami searches?

Yes, for nearby searchers, since Maps uses distance more than city names. Use your real city in your profile. Add pages for nearby areas you serve, with real details about your work there. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### How do I compete with businesses in Broward County?

Focus on the Miami-Dade areas closest to you. Strong reviews, accurate categories and helpful local pages give Google reasons to show you. If you serve Broward, say so on a page of its own. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Denver?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Denver businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Denver work. We do not have a Denver office.

### Should a Denver business create pages for Aurora or Lakewood?

Yes, if you serve customers there. They are separate cities and people search them by name. Write pages that describe your real work in each one, rather than copying the same page with a new name. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Should I mention my neighborhood, like RiNo or Cherry Creek?

Yes. Locals and visitors search by neighborhood in Denver. Use the name on your site and in posts, but keep your Business Profile name as your real business name, without added keywords. Adding special hours in your Business Profile before busy dates keeps Google Maps and AI answers from showing the wrong times.

### How do I compete in the Tech Center office market?

Pick the most accurate primary category and fill every profile field. Publish pages that answer the questions your clients ask. Collect reviews that mention the services you provide. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

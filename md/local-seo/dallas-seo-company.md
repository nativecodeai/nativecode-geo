## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Dallas?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Dallas businesses. We run audits and planning over video calls, get access to your Google Business Profile and site, and use your real photos and job details so your listings reflect actual Dallas work. We do not have a Dallas office.

### How do I rank in Google Maps in Dallas?

Start with a complete Google Business Profile at your real address, the right primary category and steady, recent reviews. Then build a page on your site for each service and for the Dallas areas you really serve. Maps favors businesses close to the searcher, so placement varies across the metro and is never guaranteed.

### Can a Dallas business rank in Plano and Frisco too?

In organic results, yes, if you build useful pages for Plano and Frisco with real projects and details. In Google Maps, distance matters, so a Dallas address has limited reach in Collin County. If you have a staffed location there, give it its own profile and page.

### Do Dallas businesses need Spanish SEO?

Many do. Dallas has a large Spanish-speaking population, so service pages written by a fluent speaker can reach customers who search in Spanish. List Spanish as a language on your profile, tag the pages with hreflang and answer Spanish reviews in Spanish.

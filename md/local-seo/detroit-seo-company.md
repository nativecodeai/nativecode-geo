## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Detroit?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Detroit businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Detroit work. We do not have a Detroit office.

### Can a Detroit business rank in suburbs like Troy or Royal Oak?

Partly. Map results depend heavily on where the searcher is. A service-area business can list the suburbs it serves and build pages for them. A storefront usually shows most often near its address. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Should my Google profile say Detroit or my neighborhood?

Your address sets the city. Use the neighborhood, such as Corktown or Midtown, in your description, website copy and photos. Do not add it to your business name unless it is part of your real name. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Do I need Canadian directory listings for Windsor customers?

Usually not. Most Windsor shoppers use Google like everyone else. Focus on accurate hours, directions from the bridge or tunnel, and clear notes on whether you accept Canadian dollars. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

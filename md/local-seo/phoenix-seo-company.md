## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Phoenix?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Phoenix businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Phoenix work. We do not have a Phoenix office.

### Can I rank in Scottsdale if my office is in Phoenix?

You can show up for some Scottsdale searches, especially near the city line. Maps still favors businesses closer to the searcher. A Scottsdale service page that describes real work there can help in organic results. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Should I mention Ahwatukee or Arcadia on my site?

Yes, if you serve those areas. Many Phoenix residents search by neighborhood or village name. Mention the areas you serve on your service pages, with real details about the work you do there. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### How do I prepare my profile for a Phoenix summer?

Confirm your hours, add emergency service details if you offer them, and post seasonal updates. Ask recent customers for reviews. Searchers in the heat often pick the business that looks most responsive. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

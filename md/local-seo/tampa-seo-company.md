## How do Tampa customers search for local businesses?

Tampa Bay is split by the water. Hillsborough County holds Tampa and Brandon, Pinellas County holds St. Petersburg and Clearwater, and Pasco County to the north holds Wesley Chapel.

### Across the bay

Few customers want to cross the Howard Frankland Bridge for a routine service. Service businesses should build pages for the side of the bay they really reach.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Tampa?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Tampa businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Tampa work. We do not have a Tampa office.

### Can a Tampa business rank in St. Petersburg and Clearwater?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for St. Petersburg and Clearwater that describe your real work there. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, and the bay separates markets, so a Tampa address has limited map reach in Pinellas County. A staffed location there can have its own profile.

### How should a Tampa business prepare for hurricane season searches?

Update your Google Business Profile with storm hours and services before June, and keep an emergency service page current. Post updates when storms approach and mark temporary closures in your profile. That keeps Google Maps and AI answers accurate when customers need you.

### Do Tampa businesses need Spanish SEO?

Many do. Tampa has a long Cuban and Spanish history in Ybor City and West Tampa, and a large Spanish-speaking population today. Spanish service pages written by a fluent speaker, plus replies to Spanish reviews, help those customers choose you.

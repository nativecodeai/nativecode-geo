## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Richmond?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Richmond businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Richmond work. We do not have a Richmond office.

### Our office is in Midlothian. Can we rank for Richmond searches?

For searches near your office, it is possible. Searchers in the Fan or Church Hill see closer businesses first in Maps. A Richmond service page and reviews from city customers can help organic visibility, though no one can guarantee placement.

### Should our Richmond site name neighborhoods or counties?

Both, where they fit. Homeowners often search by county, like Henrico or Chesterfield, while diners and shoppers search by neighborhood. Match each page to how your customers actually describe where they are. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### Do AI tools recommend Richmond restaurants and shops?

Yes. People ask AI tools for places to eat in Carytown or things to do near the James River. Those answers draw on reviews, Business Profiles and clear website content, so keeping all three accurate matters. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

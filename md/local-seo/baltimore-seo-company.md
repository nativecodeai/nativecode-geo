## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Baltimore?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Baltimore businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Baltimore work. We do not have a Baltimore office.

### Should I mention Baltimore neighborhoods on my website?

Yes, where you have real work there. Locals often search by neighborhood, such as Canton or Hampden. Pages describing real projects or services in those areas help searchers and AI tools. Writing your service details as short, direct answers makes it easier for AI tools to quote you accurately.

### Can I rank in Towson if my office is in the city?

Partly. Map results favor businesses near the searcher, so Towson results lean toward Towson businesses. A Towson service page and reviews from customers there help, but rankings are not guaranteed. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

### Do Baltimore crab houses and restaurants need special listing work?

They benefit from current menus, seasonal hours, clear photos and replies to reviews. Visitors often search for crabs near the harbor, so accurate details matter for Maps and AI answers. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

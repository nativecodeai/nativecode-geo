## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Fort Collins?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Fort Collins businesses. We meet over video during Mountain hours, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Fort Collins work. We do not have an office there.

### Should a Fort Collins business target Northern Colorado searches?

Yes, alongside Fort Collins. Many people search for services in Northern Colorado or NoCo. Use that phrase naturally on your site and profile description, and build pages for the cities you really serve, like Loveland or Windsor, with real projects in each. Avoid thin pages that only swap the city name.

### Can a Fort Collins business rank in Loveland and Greeley?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for those cities. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so a Fort Collins address has limited reach in Greeley. Add those cities to your service area if you serve them, and give any staffed location its own profile.

### How do Fort Collins businesses reach CSU students?

Students search on their phones for food, housing and services close to campus. Keep your Google Business Profile accurate, mention nearby landmarks and streets, post updates at the start of each semester and collect reviews from student customers. Clear hours and student offers on your site help AI answers recommend you.

### How do we keep Charleston, WV separate from Charleston, SC in search?

Use Charleston, WV in title tags, headings and schema, and keep your full address and ZIP code identical everywhere. Mention local places like the Kanawha River or Capitol Market so search engines and AI tools place you correctly. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

### Can a Charleston business rank in Hurricane or Teays Valley?

Partly. Map results favor nearby businesses, so a downtown address has limited reach into Putnam County. A service area that includes those towns, a useful page about your work there and reviews from customers there can help. Results are not guaranteed.

### Do we need separate listings for Charleston and South Charleston?

Only if you have a staffed location in each. South Charleston is its own city, so if your office is there, use that city name in your address and citations. Then target Charleston searches with page content, not a second address.

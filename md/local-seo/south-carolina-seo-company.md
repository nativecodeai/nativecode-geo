## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in South Carolina?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across South Carolina. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real South Carolina work. We do not have an office in South Carolina.

### Should a Myrtle Beach business change its profile for the season?

Yes. Update hours, services and photos before summer, and again when the season slows. Posts about seasonal offers keep your profile active. Accurate hours prevent bad reviews from visitors who find you closed. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

### Do I need separate pages for Greenville and Spartanburg?

If you serve both, yes. They are separate cities with their own map results. Write each page with real local details, such as projects, directions or service hours, rather than copying text. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Can AI tools recommend my South Carolina business?

They can. Tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews answer questions such as where to get shrimp and grits in Charleston. They use websites, reviews and listings. Clear, accurate content helps, but no one can guarantee a mention. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

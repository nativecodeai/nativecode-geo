## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in San Antonio?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with San Antonio businesses. We plan over video calls, make changes directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real San Antonio work. We do not have an office there.

### Do San Antonio businesses need Spanish SEO?

Most consumer businesses benefit from it. Many San Antonio customers search in Spanish or switch between languages. Have a fluent speaker write your key service pages, tag them with hreflang, list Spanish on your profile and reply to Spanish reviews in Spanish.

### How can I reach military families moving to San Antonio?

Build a page that speaks to service members arriving at Joint Base San Antonio, with practical details like the areas you serve near Lackland, Randolph or Fort Sam Houston. Keep your profile accurate, collect reviews and answer common moving questions plainly so AI answers can quote you.

### Can a San Antonio business rank in New Braunfels or Boerne?

In organic results, yes, with pages for those cities that describe real work there. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so a San Antonio address has limited reach in Comal or Kendall County. If you have a staffed location there, give it its own profile.

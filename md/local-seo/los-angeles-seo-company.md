## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Los Angeles?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Los Angeles businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Los Angeles work. We do not have a Los Angeles office.

### Should I target Los Angeles or my neighborhood?

Both. Your profile reflects your real address, and Maps shows you to people nearby. Mention your neighborhood on your site, and build pages for nearby areas you serve, like the Westside or the Valley. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

### Can a Santa Monica business show up for Los Angeles searches?

Yes, for searchers nearby, since Maps uses distance more than city lines. Santa Monica is its own city, so use its name on your profile and pages. Add Westside pages if you serve those areas. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### Do I need Spanish content in Los Angeles?

For many LA businesses, yes. Spanish is widely spoken across the city. Add pages written by fluent speakers, list languages spoken on your profile and answer Spanish reviews in Spanish. Keep your business name, address and phone number identical in every language version so search engines treat them as one business.

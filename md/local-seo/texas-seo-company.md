## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Texas?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Texas. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Texas work. We do not have an office in Texas.

### Can one Texas company rank in both Dallas and Houston?

In organic results, it can, with strong pages for each city. In Google Maps, you need a real, staffed location in each metro. Virtual offices used only to appear in a city break Google's guidelines and risk suspension. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### Should we build Spanish versions of our Texas location pages?

If your customers search in Spanish, yes. Write the pages properly in Spanish rather than running them through a translator, and tag them with hreflang. This is common in San Antonio, the Rio Grande Valley and Houston. Use the same business name, address and phone number on every language version of a page, so Google sees one business, not two.

### Do Texas suburbs like Frisco or Katy need their own pages?

If you serve them and have real work there, they often do. Customers in Frisco, Plano, Katy or Round Rock search by their own city name. A page with local projects and service details gives Google something specific to show.

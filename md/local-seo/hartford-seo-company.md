## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Hartford?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Hartford businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Hartford work. We do not have a Hartford office.

### Should I target Hartford or West Hartford?

Use your real town in your profile, since that is where Maps places you. If you serve both, build a page for the other town that describes real work there. Customers here often search by town name. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Do I need Spanish content in Hartford?

Many Hartford customers speak Spanish, so it often helps. Add pages written by fluent speakers, list Spanish as a spoken language if your staff speak it, and reply to Spanish reviews in Spanish. Use the same business name, address and phone number on every language version of a page, so Google sees one business, not two.

### Can I reach customers near Springfield, Massachusetts?

If you serve them, yes. Towns north of Hartford sit close to Springfield. List the Massachusetts areas you cover, note any licenses you hold there and add a page about your work in that area. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

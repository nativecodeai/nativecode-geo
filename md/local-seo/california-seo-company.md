## How do California customers search for local businesses?

California is many local search markets, not one. Los Angeles, San Diego, the Bay Area, Sacramento and the Inland Empire each behave like separate markets. The Central Valley, from Bakersfield to Fresno to Stockton, has its own customers.

01

### Multilingual searches

Many Californians search in Spanish, and some communities search in Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese or Tagalog. Pages in your customers' languages can widen your reach.

02

### Central Valley agriculture

Farms and food processors around Fresno and Bakersfield rely on equipment dealers, repair shops and suppliers. Those businesses often serve wide rural areas, not one city.

03

### Bay Area density

In San Francisco, Oakland and San Jose, many competitors sit within a few blocks. Small details like categories, hours and photos often separate one listing from another.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in California?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across California. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real California work. We do not have an office in California.

### Should a Southern California business target the Bay Area too?

Only if you truly serve it. The regions are hundreds of miles apart and Maps treats them as separate markets. A real location in each region needs its own profile and page. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Do I need pages in Spanish or other languages?

If many of your customers use another language, yes. Write those pages for people and have a fluent speaker review them. Mention the languages your staff speak in your profile and on your site. Use the same business name, address and phone number on every language version of a page, so Google sees one business, not two.

### Can a business rank in many California cities at once?

Organic results can reach many cities, but Maps stays close to your address. Build pages only for cities where you do real work. Thin pages that swap in city names tend to perform poorly. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

## How do New Jersey customers search for local businesses?

New Jersey search pulls toward New York or Philadelphia. Newark, Jersey City and Paterson anchor the north, while Camden and Cherry Hill face Philadelphia. Central Jersey towns like New Brunswick and Trenton sit in between.

### The Jersey Shore season

Shore towns like Asbury Park, Long Beach Island and Cape May fill up in summer. Rentals, restaurants and shops should set summer hours before the season starts.

02

### Dense towns close together

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in New Jersey?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across New Jersey. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real New Jersey work. We do not have an office in New Jersey.

### Should a Cherry Hill business target Philadelphia?

If you serve Philadelphia customers, yes. Build a page about that work and list it as a service area. Your profile address stays in New Jersey, so reviews from Philadelphia customers help support it. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### How do I handle many small towns in my service area?

List the towns you actually serve, within Google's limit, and build pages for your main ones. Each page should include real details like jobs done there, local photos and travel times. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### When should Shore businesses update their profiles?

Before summer, update hours, menus, photos and booking links. Then set special hours for the off-season. Many visitors check Google Maps on the drive down, so accuracy matters. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Ohio?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Ohio. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Ohio work. We do not have an office in Ohio.

### I have locations in Cleveland and Columbus. Do I need separate pages?

Yes. Each location should have its own Google Business Profile and its own page with that address, hours, services and local details. Separate pages give Google and AI tools a clear match for each city. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

### What citation sites matter for Ohio businesses?

Start with Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and industry directories. Then add regional sources, such as your local chamber of commerce. Your name, address and phone number should match everywhere. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### Can AI answers include my Ohio business?

They can. Tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews answer questions such as where to get brunch in Cleveland. They use websites, reviews and listings, so accurate and detailed content helps. No one can guarantee inclusion. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

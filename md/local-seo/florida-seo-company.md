Searching in Florida

## How do Florida customers search for local businesses?

Florida search changes with visitors, snowbirds and storms. South Florida, Tampa Bay, Central Florida and Jacksonville are separate markets. The Panhandle around Pensacola and the Southwest coast around Fort Myers and Naples have their own customers.

01

### Hurricane season searches

From June to November, searches for roofers, generators, tree removal and storm repair can rise when storms threaten. Emergency hours and fast replies matter then.

02

### Theme park visitors in Orlando

Visitors to Orlando's theme parks search for food, rentals and things to do nearby. Businesses near International Drive and Kissimmee need listings built for first time visitors.

03

### Winter residents

Snowbirds arrive in the cooler months, especially in Southwest Florida and South Florida. Health care, dining and home services see demand shift with them.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Florida?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Florida. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Florida work. We do not have an office in Florida.

### Do I need Spanish content for Florida customers?

In many Florida markets, yes. South Florida, Orlando and Tampa all have large Spanish speaking communities. Add pages written by fluent speakers and list Spanish as a language spoken if your staff speak it. Keep your business name, address and phone number identical in every language version so search engines treat them as one business.

### How do I update my profile during a hurricane?

Set special hours if you close, and post updates as conditions change. If you offer storm services, say so clearly and keep your phone line answered. Remove temporary notices once you reopen. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Can one Florida business rank in Tampa and Orlando?

Maps treats them as separate markets. If you have locations in both, give each its own profile and page. Without a location, a service page can still help in organic results. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

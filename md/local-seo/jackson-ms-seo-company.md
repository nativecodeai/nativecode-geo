## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Jackson?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Jackson businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Jackson work. We do not have a Jackson office.

### Can a Jackson business rank in Madison or Flowood searches?

It depends on whether you are a storefront or a service-area business. Service businesses can list those suburbs and build real pages for them. Storefronts usually appear most often for nearby searchers. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### How do I make sure Google knows I mean Jackson, Mississippi?

Use your full address with MS on your site and listings. Add LocalBusiness schema with your state and coordinates. Consistent citations on Mississippi directories also help. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

### Do reviews matter in a smaller metro?

Yes. When competitors have few reviews, a steady flow of recent, detailed ones can set you apart. Ask customers soon after the job, and reply to every review you get. Make sure your primary Business Profile category matches your main service, since categories strongly shape which searches you appear in.

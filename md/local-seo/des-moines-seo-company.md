## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Des Moines?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Des Moines businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Des Moines work. We do not have a Des Moines office.

### Should I target West Des Moines separately from Des Moines?

Yes, if you serve it. West Des Moines is its own city, and residents often search with that name. A dedicated page with real details helps you match those searches. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### My business is in Ankeny. Can I show up in Des Moines searches?

For broad searches you may, but map results favor businesses near the searcher. Pages for the areas you serve and steady reviews help, though no one can promise map rankings. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### Do Des Moines neighborhoods matter for local SEO?

They can. People search for things like a coffee shop in Beaverdale or a dentist near Drake. Mentioning real neighborhoods you serve helps Google and AI tools place you accurately. Writing your service details as short, direct answers makes it easier for AI tools to quote you accurately.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Philadelphia?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Philadelphia businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Philadelphia work. We do not have a Philadelphia office.

### Should I mention my neighborhood on my website?

Yes, where it is true. People search for things like a pizza place in Fishtown or a dentist in Center City. Naming your neighborhood in page titles, content and schema helps match those searches. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Can my Philadelphia business rank in Cherry Hill?

It can rank in regular search results with a strong page about your work in Cherry Hill. Map results there depend on distance, so they are harder without a New Jersey location. We focus on areas you truly serve. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### What citations matter for Philadelphia businesses?

Start with Google, Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp and your industry directories. Add regional ones, such as neighborhood business associations. Keep your name, address and phone number identical everywhere, including suite numbers. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

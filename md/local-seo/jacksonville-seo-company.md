## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Jacksonville?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Jacksonville businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Jacksonville work. We do not have an office there.

### Do Jacksonville businesses need pages for each part of town?

Often, yes, because the city is so large. A business on the Southside may not show in Maps for searchers in Arlington or the Westside. Build pages for the areas you really serve, with real projects and details, and keep your service area accurate.

### Can a Jacksonville business rank in St. Johns or Clay County?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for Ponte Vedra, St. Augustine or Orange Park that describe your real work there. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so a staffed location in those counties can have its own profile and page.

### How should Jacksonville businesses prepare for storm season?

Update your Google Business Profile with storm hours and services before hurricane season, and keep an emergency service page current. Post updates when storms approach and mark temporary closures, then reopen them promptly. That keeps Google Maps and AI answers accurate when customers need help most.

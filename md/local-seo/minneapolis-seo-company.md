## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Minneapolis?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Minneapolis businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Minneapolis work. We do not have a Minneapolis office.

### Will a Minneapolis address help me rank in Edina or Bloomington?

Somewhat, but map results favor businesses close to the searcher. Service-area businesses can list those suburbs and build real pages for them. Storefronts benefit from reviews and links from nearby customers. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### Should I mention the skyway in my Google profile?

Yes, if customers can reach you through it. Add it to your description and website directions, and post photos of the entrance. This helps people find you in winter, when the skyway is busiest. Set special hours in your Google Business Profile ahead of time so Maps and AI answers do not show outdated hours on busy days.

### Does Google treat Minneapolis and St. Paul as one market?

No. They are separate cities, and searches that name one city usually favor businesses there. If you serve both, make that clear with a page for each that reflects real work. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with organizations in Washington, DC?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses and organizations in Washington, DC. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and details so your listings show real DC work. We do not have a DC office.

### How do DC federal contractors and associations get found in search?

Buyers and members search by capability, topic and contract need. Build a page for each capability or program with clear scope and qualifications, keep your organization facts consistent across your site and profiles and answer common questions plainly so AI answers can cite you.

### Can a DC business rank in Arlington or Bethesda searches?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for those areas that describe your real work there. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so a DC address mostly shows to nearby searchers. A staffed office in Virginia or Maryland can have its own profile.

### Should a DC business target its neighborhood or the DMV?

Both, depending on how far customers travel. Restaurants and shops should name their neighborhood, like Navy Yard or Dupont Circle, while service firms that cover the region can add a DMV page. Build pages only for areas you really serve.

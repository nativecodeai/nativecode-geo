## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Salt Lake City?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Salt Lake City businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Salt Lake City work. We do not have a Salt Lake City office.

### We are in downtown Salt Lake. Can we reach customers in Draper or Lehi?

Map results favor businesses near the searcher, so a downtown address has limited pull in Draper or Lehi. If you serve those areas, list them in your service area and build specific pages. Map placement is never guaranteed. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### How should we write grid addresses on our listings?

Pick one format, such as 1300 E or 1300 East, and use it everywhere: Google, Apple Maps, Yelp and your site footer. Consistent formatting helps search engines match your citations to one business. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Do Salt Lake businesses get much search traffic from tourists?

Many do. Visitors come for conventions, the canyons and Temple Square, and they often ask AI tools for nearby options. Clear hours, menus and photos on your profile and site help those answers include you. Writing your service details as short, direct answers makes it easier for AI tools to quote you accurately.

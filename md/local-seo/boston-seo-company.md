## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Boston?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Boston businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Boston work. We do not have a Boston office.

### Should I target Cambridge and Somerville separately from Boston?

Yes, if you serve them. They are separate cities, and residents often search with those names. A page for each with real service details helps Google and AI tools match you. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

### Can I rank across Boston with one location?

Partly. Map results cover a small radius in dense areas, so one location shows strongest nearby. Neighborhood pages and organic content can reach further, but citywide rankings are not promised. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### How should student turnover affect my Boston marketing?

Plan for late summer move-in and spring move-out. Update services and posts before those periods, and keep collecting reviews so new residents see current feedback. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Birmingham?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Birmingham businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Birmingham work. We do not have a Birmingham office.

### Should I target Birmingham or the suburb where my office is?

Target both, but be honest about your address. Your Business Profile should show your real location. Service pages for places like Hoover or Homewood can explain how you serve them without claiming an office there. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Does being in Shelby County hurt my Birmingham visibility?

Not by itself. Google weighs distance from the searcher, so a Pelham or Chelsea business may show for nearby searches but less for downtown ones. Strong reviews and a complete profile help at the edges of that range. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### What should a medical practice near UAB do first?

Verify your Business Profile, pick the most accurate primary category and list each provider correctly. Add parking and entrance details, since the Southside can be confusing for new patients. Then add schema to your location page. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

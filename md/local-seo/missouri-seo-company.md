## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Missouri?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Missouri. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Missouri work. We do not have an office in Missouri.

### Should a St. Louis business target Illinois customers?

If you serve them, yes. Build a page about the Metro East areas you cover and list them as service areas. Get listed in directories on both sides of the river so your details match. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### How should a Lake of the Ozarks business handle winter?

Keep your profile open with accurate special hours rather than marking it closed. Post updates about reopening and booking. People plan summer lake trips months ahead, so steady information helps. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Is it worth having pages for small towns I serve?

Yes, if you do real work there. Each page should include specifics like services offered, travel area and local photos. Avoid copying one page and swapping town names, since Google tends to ignore those. Choosing the most accurate primary category in your Google Business Profile is one of the simplest ways to match more searches.

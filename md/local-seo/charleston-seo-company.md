## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Charleston?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Charleston businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Charleston work. We do not have a Charleston office.

### How do I reach visitors planning a trip to Charleston?

Many visitors search weeks before they arrive. Clear pages about what you offer, current hours and strong photos help. AI tools often answer trip planning questions, and they draw on the same content and reviews. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

### Should my business target Mount Pleasant separately?

If you serve customers there, yes. Mount Pleasant is its own town with its own map results. A page with real details about your work there, plus service areas on your profile, helps you match those searches. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### Does hurricane season affect local search in Charleston?

Yes. Searches for storm prep, tree work, roofing and water damage repair rise during the season. Updating hours during storms and posting service updates keeps customers informed and your profile accurate. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

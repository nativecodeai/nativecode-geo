## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Newark?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Newark businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Newark work. We do not have a Newark office.

### Should my site be in Portuguese or Spanish?

If many customers speak those languages, yes. Add translated pages written for real people, not machine copy. Note the languages you speak in your profile, and make sure staff can respond. Use the same business name, address and phone number on every language version of a page, so Google sees one business, not two.

### Can a Newark business show up in Jersey City or Elizabeth searches?

Service-area businesses can list those cities and build pages for them. Storefronts tend to show most for nearby searchers. Reviews and links from customers in those cities can help a little. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### Do I need to separate Newark from New York searches?

Newark sits in the New York metro, so broad searches can show results from across the river. Using Newark, NJ clearly in titles, schema and listings helps Google place you. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

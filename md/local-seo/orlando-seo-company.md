## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Orlando?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Orlando businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings reflect real Orlando work. We do not have an Orlando office.

### How do Orlando tourism businesses get found by visitors?

Visitors search on their phones near where they are staying, so your Google Business Profile matters most. Keep hours, photos, menus and booking links current, choose precise categories and reply to reviews. Pages that answer common visitor questions help AI answers recommend you.

### Can a Kissimmee or Winter Park business rank for Orlando searches?

Yes, for nearby searchers, since Google Maps uses distance more than city names. Use your real city on your profile and site. Add pages for nearby areas you serve, with real details about your work there, so organic results can show you too.

### Do Orlando businesses need Spanish SEO?

Many do. Central Florida has a large Spanish-speaking population, especially in Osceola County. Have a fluent speaker write key service pages, tag them with hreflang, list Spanish on your profile and reply to Spanish reviews in Spanish. Machine translation alone tends to read poorly and earns less trust.

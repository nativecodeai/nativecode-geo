## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Chicago?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Chicago businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Chicago work. We do not have a Chicago office.

### Can I rank across Chicago with one location?

Partly. Map results in Chicago cover a small radius, so one storefront will show strongest in its own area. Neighborhood pages and organic content can reach further, but rankings in every neighborhood cannot be promised. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Should I target neighborhood names like Wicker Park or Lakeview?

Yes, where you have a real connection. Chicagoans search by neighborhood often. Pages that describe real work in Wicker Park or Lakeview are useful. Thin pages that only swap names are not. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Do suburbs like Evanston and Oak Park need separate pages?

If you serve them regularly, a page for each is helpful. Evanston and Oak Park are separate municipalities, and searchers there often add the suburb name rather than Chicago. Choosing the most accurate primary category in your Google Business Profile is one of the simplest ways to match more searches.

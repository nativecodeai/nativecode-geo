## How do Las Vegas customers search for local businesses?

Las Vegas search is split between visitors and residents. Residents search from Summerlin, Spring Valley, Enterprise, Henderson and North Las Vegas. Visitors search from the Strip and Downtown, often on their phones, for food and things to do.

01

### The Strip sits outside city limits

Much of the Strip is in unincorporated Clark County, not the City of Las Vegas. Addresses and listings should match how Google and the county record them.

02

### Convention crowds

Large conventions fill hotels near the Strip several times a year. Restaurants, transport and printing services nearby can post updates timed to big shows.

03

### Bilingual searches in Las Vegas

Many Las Vegas residents search in Spanish, especially in East Las Vegas and North Las Vegas. Spanish pages and staff who can take calls in Spanish help.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Las Vegas?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Las Vegas businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Las Vegas work. We do not have a Las Vegas office.

### Should I target tourists or locals?

That depends on who buys from you. Tourist businesses need visibility around the Strip and Downtown, with quick facts like hours and reservations. Local services need pages for areas like Summerlin and Henderson. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### Is Henderson a separate market from Las Vegas?

Yes. Henderson is its own city, and searches that name it favor businesses there. If you serve Henderson, list it as a service area and build a page that reflects real work there. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Does my business need to show up in AI answers for visitors?

Many visitors ask AI assistants where to eat or what to do. Those tools draw on your Google profile, website and reviews. Clear menus, hours and descriptions give them accurate details to share. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

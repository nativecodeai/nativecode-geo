## How do New York customers search for local businesses?

In New York City, distance is measured in blocks, not miles. Searchers in Midtown, Williamsburg, Astoria or Flushing see different map results for the same query. The metro also reaches into New Jersey, Westchester and Long Island, where many commuters search.

01

### Neighborhood-level competition

A dentist on the Upper West Side competes with offices a few blocks away, not across the city. Neighborhood names in pages and profiles help match how people search.

02

### Searches in many languages

Spanish and Chinese searches are common in neighborhoods such as Washington Heights, Sunset Park and Flushing. Businesses serving these customers benefit from pages in those languages.

03

### Visitors searching near landmarks

Tourists search for food and services near Times Square, the Financial District and Central Park. Accurate hours, menus and photos matter when someone is deciding from a phone on the street.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in New York City?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with New York businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real New York work. We do not have a New York office.

### Should I list my borough or my neighborhood on my website?

Use both where they are true. Mention the borough and the neighborhood in page titles, content and schema. People search for terms like plumber in Park Slope and plumber in Brooklyn, and your pages should match both. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

### Can a service business in Queens show up in Manhattan map results?

A service area business can list the areas it serves, but Google still weighs distance to the searcher. Ranking in a borough where you have no address is harder. Pages for each area you truly serve can help in regular results.

### Do customers in New Jersey and Westchester find NYC businesses in search?

Many do, especially commuters searching near where they work. If you serve customers from Hoboken, Jersey City or Westchester, say so on your site and in your service areas. Never create fake addresses there. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

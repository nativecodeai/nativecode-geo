## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Raleigh?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Raleigh businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Raleigh work. We do not have a Raleigh office.

### Can a Raleigh business rank in Durham and Chapel Hill?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for Durham and Chapel Hill that describe your real work there. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so a Raleigh address has limited reach in Durham and Orange counties. A staffed location there can have its own profile.

### Should a Raleigh business target the Triangle or Raleigh?

Both. Some people search for the Triangle as a region, while most search with a city name like Raleigh, Cary or Apex. Use your real address on your profile, build pages for the cities you serve and use a Triangle page as a hub.

### How do Triangle tech and life science firms get cited in AI answers?

AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer buyer questions directly. Publish pages for each service or product use case, keep company facts consistent across your site and profiles and add structured data so tools can read them.

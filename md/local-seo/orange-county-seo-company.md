### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Orange County?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Orange County businesses. We meet over video during Pacific hours, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Orange County work. We do not have an office there.

### Should I target Orange County or my city in SEO?

Target your city first. Most people search with a city name, like Irvine or Huntington Beach, and Google Maps favors nearby businesses. Build pages for the cities you really serve, and use an Orange County page as a hub that links to them.

### Is Vietnamese SEO worth it in Orange County?

For many businesses near Westminster and Garden Grove, yes. If your staff serve customers in Vietnamese, add pages written by a fluent speaker, list Vietnamese on your profile and reply to Vietnamese reviews in Vietnamese. The same approach works for Spanish and Korean.

### Can an Orange County business rank in Los Angeles searches?

In organic results, it can, with a useful page about your work in Los Angeles. In Google Maps, distance matters, so an Orange County address will mostly show to nearby searchers. A real staffed location in Los Angeles County can have its own profile.

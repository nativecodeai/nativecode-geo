## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Oklahoma City?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Oklahoma City businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Oklahoma City work. We do not have an Oklahoma City office.

### Should I build pages for Edmond, Moore and Norman?

If you serve customers in those cities, yes. Write each page with real details for that area, not the same text with a new name. Thin, copied pages can hurt more than they help. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Should my Oklahoma City website have Spanish pages?

If you serve Spanish-speaking customers, it can help. Many customers on the south side search in Spanish. Write real Spanish pages instead of machine translations, and note on your profile that you offer service in Spanish. Use the same business name, address and phone number on every language version of a page, so Google sees one business, not two.

### How do reviews affect map rankings in Oklahoma City?

Reviews are part of how Google judges prominence, along with relevance and distance. Recent, genuine reviews that mention your services help. We set up request steps that follow Google's rules and never buy or filter reviews. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

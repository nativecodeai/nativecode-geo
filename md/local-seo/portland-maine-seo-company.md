## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Portland?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Portland businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Portland work. We do not have a Portland office.

### How do I keep my business from being confused with Portland, Oregon?

Use Portland, Maine in page titles, headings and your footer address. Add LocalBusiness schema with the ME state code. Consistent listings on directories help AI tools place you in Maine. Writing your service details as short, direct answers makes it easier for AI tools to quote you accurately.

### Should I target South Portland and Scarborough too?

If you serve them, yes. South Portland and Scarborough are separate municipalities, and residents often search by those names. A page per area with real details helps. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### How should I handle the slower winter months?

Update hours if they change, post winter offers and keep collecting reviews from regular local customers. Steady activity keeps your listing fresh until summer traffic returns. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

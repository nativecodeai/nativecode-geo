Searching on Long Island

## How do Long Island customers search for local businesses?

Long Island customers search by town, village and hamlet, such as Garden City, Massapequa, Huntington or Patchogue. Nassau and Suffolk counties are separate markets with different drive times.

01

### Hamlets and villages

Long Island has many hamlets and villages, and people search with those names. Pages for the places you really serve help you show up when people search by hamlet.

02

### Hauppauge and Melville business districts

The Hauppauge Industrial Park and the Route 110 corridor in Melville hold many manufacturers and offices. B2B firms there need pages for each service they offer.

03

### The East End season

The Hamptons and the North Fork wineries get busy in summer. Restaurants, rentals and home services there should update hours and photos before the season.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses on Long Island?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Long Island businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Long Island work. We do not have an office there.

### Should a Long Island business target its town or Long Island?

Both. Many people search with a town or hamlet name, while others search for Long Island in general. Use your real address on your profile, build pages for the towns you really serve and use a Long Island page as a hub that links to them.

### Can a Nassau County business rank in Suffolk County?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for the Suffolk towns you serve. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so a Nassau address has limited reach farther east. If you have a staffed Suffolk location, give it its own profile and page.

### How should East End businesses prepare for summer searches?

Update your Google Business Profile hours, photos and services before Memorial Day, and keep booking links current. Post about seasonal hours and events. Visitors and summer residents search on their phones and rely on Maps and AI answers to choose.

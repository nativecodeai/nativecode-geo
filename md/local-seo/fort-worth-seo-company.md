## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Fort Worth?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Fort Worth businesses. We plan over video, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Fort Worth work. We do not have a Fort Worth office.

### Should a Fort Worth business target Dallas searches too?

Only if you really serve Dallas customers. Fort Worth and Dallas are separate markets in Google Maps, so a Fort Worth address will mostly show to nearby searchers. You can still rank organically for Dallas with a useful page about your work there, but a map listing needs a real staffed location.

### How do I rank in Arlington and the Mid-Cities from Fort Worth?

Add Arlington, Hurst, Euless and Bedford to your service area if you serve them, then build a page for each area with real projects and details. Maps favors nearby businesses, so results in those cities depend on distance from your address. Reviews that mention the area also help.

### What should Fort Worth B2B companies focus on in SEO?

Focus on service pages that match what buyers search, such as precision machining or freight services, plus clear proof of capability. Defense and logistics buyers research before they call, so pages for each service, case studies and accurate profiles matter more than a single city keyword.

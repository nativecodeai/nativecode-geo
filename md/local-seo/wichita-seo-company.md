## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Wichita?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Wichita businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Wichita work. We do not have a Wichita office.

### I'm on the west side. Can I show up for east Wichita searches?

Map results weigh distance, so an east side searcher may see closer businesses first. Clear service areas, east side project pages and reviews can help, but map rankings cannot be promised. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

### Should I build pages for Derby and Andover?

If you serve them, yes. Residents there often search with their own city name. One page per suburb with real details helps searchers and AI tools understand your reach. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

### How do I handle storm-related searches without looking spammy?

Write one solid page per service, like hail damage roof repair, with real Wichita details. Avoid copying pages for every town. Keep your Google Business Profile updated as demand changes. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in New Mexico?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across New Mexico. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real New Mexico work. We do not have an office in New Mexico.

### Should a Las Cruces business target El Paso?

If you serve El Paso customers, yes. Build a page for that work and list it as a service area. Texas directory listings and reviews from El Paso customers help support your reach across the line. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

### Do I need my website in Spanish?

If many of your customers prefer Spanish, it helps. Write Spanish pages for people, not machine translations. Add Spanish details to your profile and make sure someone can answer calls in Spanish. Use the same business name, address and phone number on every language version of a page, so Google sees one business, not two.

### How do Santa Fe businesses handle tourist seasons?

Update hours, photos and posts before busy summer months and the holidays. Keep the profile accurate in quieter months too, since many visitors plan trips and check listings early. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

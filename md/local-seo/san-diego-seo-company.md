## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in San Diego?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with San Diego businesses. We meet over video during Pacific hours, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real San Diego work. We do not have a San Diego office.

### Should a San Diego business target the city or its neighborhood?

Both. Google Maps uses your real address and shows you to nearby searchers, while many people search by community, like La Jolla or North Park. Name your community on your site and build pages for the nearby areas you really serve.

### Can a San Diego business rank in North County?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for Carlsbad, Oceanside or Escondido that describe your real work there. In Google Maps, distance matters, so a downtown address has limited reach up the coast. A staffed North County location can have its own profile and page.

### Do San Diego businesses need Spanish SEO?

Many do, especially in the South Bay and for businesses that serve cross-border customers. Have a fluent speaker write key service pages, tag them with hreflang, list Spanish on your profile and reply to Spanish reviews in Spanish. Clear directions from the border crossings also help.

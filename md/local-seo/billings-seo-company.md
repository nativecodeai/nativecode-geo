## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Billings?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Billings businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Billings work. We do not have a Billings office.

### Can a Billings business show up for searches in nearby towns?

Often, since rural areas have fewer competitors. Service businesses should list the towns they serve. Pages that describe real work in places like Laurel or Hardin can help. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Should I mention Wyoming if customers come from there?

If you serve them, yes. A page about serving towns like Sheridan or Cody helps. Your profile address stays in Montana, but your site and reviews can show the wider area you cover. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

### How important is my Google Business Profile in Billings?

Very. Many local searches end in the map pack or a call straight from the profile. Accurate hours, categories, photos and review replies give customers and AI assistants what they need. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

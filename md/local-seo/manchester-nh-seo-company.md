## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Manchester?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Manchester businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Manchester work. We do not have a Manchester office.

### Should I target Bedford and Hooksett too?

If you serve those towns, list them as service areas and build pages that describe real work there. Storefronts tend to show most for nearby searchers, so reviews from those towns also help. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### How do I avoid being confused with Manchester, England?

Use Manchester, NH in your title tags, footer and contact page. Add LocalBusiness schema with your full address. Keep your citations consistent on New Hampshire and New England directories. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### Do Elm Street businesses need parking details?

It helps. Downtown customers often decide based on parking. Add garage or street parking notes to your profile and website, and post a photo of your entrance so people find you. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Fargo?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Fargo businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Fargo work. We do not have a Fargo office.

### Should my Fargo business list Moorhead as a service area?

If you serve Moorhead customers, yes. Add it to your service areas and mention it on your site. Minnesota customers may also check Minnesota directories, so make sure your listing details match there. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### How do I show up for near me searches in West Fargo?

Near me results depend mostly on distance, relevance and prominence. A complete profile, the right categories and recent reviews help. If you are based in Fargo, a West Fargo page can still support regular search results. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

### Does the season change what Fargo customers search for?

Yes. Snow removal, heating and auto repair searches rise in winter, while lawn care, roofing and patio dining rise in summer. Updating posts, hours and services with the season keeps your profile relevant. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

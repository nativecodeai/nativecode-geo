## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in St. Louis?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with St. Louis businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real St. Louis work. We do not have a St. Louis office.

### Can a St. Louis business rank in the Illinois Metro East?

Yes, for nearby searchers, since Google Maps uses distance more than state lines. Add Belleville, O'Fallon or Edwardsville to your service area if you serve them, and build a Metro East page with real projects there. Keep Illinois licensing details clear if they apply.

### Should I target St. Louis city or St. Louis County?

Target where your customers are. Many people search with a suburb name, like Clayton or Chesterfield, while others search for St. Louis. Use your real address on your profile and build pages for the city areas and county suburbs you really serve.

### How do St. Louis healthcare practices get found?

Patients search by specialty, symptom and location. Build a page for each provider and service, keep hours, insurance details and suite numbers accurate and collect steady reviews. Clear answers to common patient questions, written in plain language, help Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT cite your practice.

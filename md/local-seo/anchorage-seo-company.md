## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Anchorage?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Anchorage businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Anchorage work. We do not have an Anchorage office.

### Can I rank in Wasilla and Palmer from Anchorage?

Partly. Maps results favor nearby businesses, so an Anchorage address will show less often for searches in the Valley. A clear service area and a Mat-Su page on your site can help you appear in organic results there. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

### How do I handle winter hours in Google?

Set regular hours for winter and use special hours for holidays and weather closures. Post updates when conditions change. Accurate hours reduce wasted trips and frustrated reviews. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### Is it worth targeting summer visitors and locals separately?

Often, yes. Visitors search for tours, rentals and things to do near downtown. Locals search for services near home. Separate pages and posts let you speak to each group without mixing messages. Set special hours in your Google Business Profile ahead of time so Maps and AI answers do not show outdated hours on busy days.

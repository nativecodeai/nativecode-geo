## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Albuquerque?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Albuquerque businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Albuquerque work. We do not have an Albuquerque office.

### Should I list Rio Rancho as a separate area?

Yes, if you serve it. Rio Rancho is its own city, and searches naming it favor businesses there. Add it as a service area and build a page that reflects real work you do there. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

### How should I prepare for Balloon Fiesta?

Update hours and photos and post event details a few weeks ahead. Make sure menus and booking links work. Visitors often search on their phones, so mobile pages need to load fast. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Does Spanish content help in Albuquerque?

For many businesses, yes. Many Albuquerque families speak Spanish at home. Spanish pages written by fluent speakers and a bilingual profile description can make you easier to choose. Keep your business name, address and phone number identical in every language version so search engines treat them as one business.

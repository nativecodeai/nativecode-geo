## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Omaha?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Omaha businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Omaha work. We do not have an Omaha office.

### Should I mention neighborhoods like Benson or Dundee?

Yes, if you are in them or serve them. Locals search by district name. Use it in your description, page copy and photo captions, but keep your business name exactly as it appears on your sign. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Can a Midtown business show up for west Omaha searches?

Partly. Map results lean toward businesses close to the searcher, so a Midtown storefront will show less in Elkhorn. Strong reviews, a complete profile and links from local sites can widen your reach somewhat. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

### Do big events like the College World Series matter for my profile?

For nearby hotels, bars and restaurants, yes. Update hours, post event details and add photos ahead of time. Visitors often check Google Maps on their phones while walking around downtown. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Montana?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Montana. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Montana work. We do not have an office in Montana.

### How far will Google show my business in rural Montana?

In less populated areas, map results can reach farther because there are fewer competitors. Distance still matters. A clear service area, consistent listings and reviews from each town all help. A short text or email with your review link, sent the same day, usually works better than asking weeks later.

### Should a Bozeman business also target Big Sky?

If you serve Big Sky, yes. Build a page about that work and list it as a service area. Big Sky has a strong seasonal market, so post updates before the ski and summer seasons. Adding special hours in your Business Profile before busy dates keeps Google Maps and AI answers from showing the wrong times.

### How should seasonal businesses handle closures?

Use special hours or mark a temporary closure rather than deleting details. Keep posting about reopening plans. Visitors often plan Glacier or Yellowstone trips months ahead and check profiles early. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

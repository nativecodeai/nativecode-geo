## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Little Rock?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Little Rock businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Little Rock work. We do not have a Little Rock office.

### Should I create a page for North Little Rock?

If you serve customers there, yes. North Little Rock is a separate city and people search it by name. Write a page about the work you do there, not a copy of your Little Rock page. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Do neighborhood names like Hillcrest help?

They can. People often search for places in the Heights, Hillcrest or SoMa. Mentioning your area, with directions and parking details, helps customers and gives AI tools clear local facts. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

### How many reviews does a Little Rock business need?

There is no fixed number. Compare yourself with the businesses that show in your map results. Aim for steady, recent reviews and reply to each one, since a slow trickle often beats a single burst. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

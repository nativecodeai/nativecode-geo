## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Columbus?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Columbus businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Columbus work. We do not have a Columbus office.

### Should I target Dublin and Westerville separately from Columbus?

If you serve them, yes. Many people search with the suburb name. Pages with real details for each area, such as projects, directions or service hours, help. Map results will still favor businesses close to the searcher. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### How do football Saturdays affect local search near campus?

Searches for food, parking, bars and hotels rise around Ohio State home games. Updating hours, posting game day specials and keeping photos current helps your profile when demand is high. Google weighs distance, relevance and prominence for local results, so a complete profile and steady reviews still matter.

### Do I need schema markup on my Columbus website?

Schema is not required, but it helps. LocalBusiness schema tells search engines and AI tools your business type, address, hours and service areas in a clear format. It supports visibility but does not guarantee it. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

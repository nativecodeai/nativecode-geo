## How do Michigan customers search for local businesses?

Michigan searches follow two peninsulas and several metros. Most demand sits in Metro Detroit, Grand Rapids, Lansing and Ann Arbor. Summer and fall add tourist searches along Lake Michigan, in Traverse City and on Mackinac Island.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Michigan?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Michigan. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Michigan work. We do not have an office in Michigan.

### Should I have separate pages for Detroit and Grand Rapids locations?

Yes, if you have a real location or real customers in each. Each page should describe that area's services, staff, directions and reviews. Thin copies with only the city name swapped tend to be ignored. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### How do I handle seasonal hours for an Up North business?

Set special hours in Google Business Profile before each season changes, and update your website at the same time. Mismatched hours confuse customers and the AI assistants that read both sources. Writing your service details as short, direct answers makes it easier for AI tools to quote you accurately.

### Does it matter if customers say the U.P. instead of the Upper Peninsula?

It can. People search both ways, so use the full name and the short form naturally on your pages. Google understands many variants, but matching how locals talk makes your copy read right. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

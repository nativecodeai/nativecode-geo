## How do Delaware customers search for local businesses?

Delaware search follows its three counties, north to south. Wilmington and Newark anchor the north, Dover sits in Kent County, and Sussex County holds the beach towns. Customers also cross in from Pennsylvania, Maryland and New Jersey.

01

### Beach season in Sussex County

Rehoboth Beach, Lewes, Dewey Beach and Bethany Beach get busy each summer. Restaurants, rentals and shops need seasonal hours and current photos before visitors arrive.

02

### No sales tax shoppers

Delaware has no sales tax, so shoppers come from nearby states. Retailers near the state lines should make store details easy to find for out of state searches.

03

### University of Delaware in Newark

Students and families visit Newark throughout the school year. Restaurants, hotels and apartment managers see searches tied to move in, events and graduation.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Delaware?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Delaware. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Delaware work. We do not have an office in Delaware.

### Can a Wilmington business reach customers in Pennsylvania?

Yes, if you serve them. Maps favors distance more than state lines, so nearby Pennsylvania searchers may see you. List those areas in your service areas and note any licenses you hold there. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### How should a Delaware beach business prepare for summer?

Update hours, photos and posts in spring, before the crowds. Answer common questions about parking, reservations and pets on your site. If you close in the off season, set those hours clearly. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Do I need separate pages for each county?

Only if you serve more than one. Customers in Sussex County search differently from those near Wilmington. A useful page for each area you serve is better than one thin page for the whole state. Choosing the most accurate primary category in your Google Business Profile is one of the simplest ways to match more searches.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Charlotte?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Charlotte businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Charlotte work. We do not have a Charlotte office.

### Should my Charlotte business target Fort Mill and Rock Hill?

If you serve customers there, it can help. Add those cities to your service areas and build a page about your work in York County. Google weighs distance, so map rankings across the state line may stay harder. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

### How many locations should be on my Google Business Profile?

Use one profile per real, staffed location. If you have offices in Ballantyne and University City, each can have its own profile. Profiles for addresses without staff or signage break Google's rules and can be suspended. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Does schema markup help Charlotte businesses in AI answers?

Schema tells search engines your business type, address, hours and services in a structured way. It does not guarantee AI mentions, but it makes your details easier to read and match. We add LocalBusiness schema to each location page. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

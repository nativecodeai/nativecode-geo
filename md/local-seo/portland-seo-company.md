## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Portland?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Portland businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Portland work. We do not have a Portland office.

### Should my Portland business target Vancouver, Washington?

If you serve customers there, yes. Add Vancouver to your service areas and write a page with real details about your work there. Map rankings across the river can be harder, since Google weighs distance. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Should I use NE, SE or other address sections in my content?

Use them where they are accurate. Portland addresses include sections like NE, SE and NW, and people know their part of town by them. Matching your address format across listings also keeps your citations consistent. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Can AI answers recommend my Portland business?

They can. Tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews answer questions such as where to find food carts in Southeast Portland. They use websites, reviews and listings. Detailed, accurate content helps, but no one can guarantee a mention. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

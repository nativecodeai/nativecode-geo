### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Fort Lauderdale?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Fort Lauderdale businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Fort Lauderdale work. We do not have an office there.

### Can a Fort Lauderdale business compete with Miami businesses in search?

Yes, for nearby searchers. Google Maps uses distance, so a Fort Lauderdale address mostly competes within Broward County. Focus on the Broward cities closest to you with strong reviews and city pages. If you serve Miami-Dade too, give that area its own page.

### How do Fort Lauderdale marine businesses get found?

Boat owners search by service and by marina or waterway. Build a page for each service, such as engine repair or detailing, name the marinas and areas you cover, and use photos of real jobs. Keep hours and contact details current during boat show season.

### Do Fort Lauderdale businesses need multilingual SEO?

Many do. Broward County has large Spanish, Portuguese and Haitian Creole speaking communities. If your staff serve customers in those languages, add pages written by fluent speakers, list the languages on your profile and reply to reviews in the language they were written in.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Houston?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Houston businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Houston work. We do not have a Houston office.

### We are in the Heights. Can we show up for searches in Katy or Sugar Land?

Maps results favor nearby businesses, so a Heights address has limited reach in Katy or Sugar Land. If you serve those suburbs, add them to your service area and build useful pages for them. Map placement is never guaranteed. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Do Houston businesses need separate pages for each side of town?

Only where your service really differs or you have locations there. A clinic with offices near the Galleria and in Clear Lake needs a profile and page for each. A single office should focus on where customers actually come from.

### How should a Houston business prepare for hurricane season searches?

Update your Business Profile with storm hours and services before the season starts, and keep an emergency service page current. Post updates when storms approach so Google and AI tools show accurate information. AI tools such as ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer a question directly, so put short, plain answers near the top.

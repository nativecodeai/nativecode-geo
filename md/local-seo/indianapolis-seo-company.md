### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Indianapolis?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Indianapolis businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Indianapolis work. We do not have an Indianapolis office.

### Should I use Indianapolis or Indy on my website?

Both. Locals search with both names. Use Indianapolis in key places like titles and your address, and let Indy appear naturally in your content where it reads well. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### I'm on the south side. Can I reach Carmel searchers?

Map results favor businesses near the searcher, so Carmel results lean north side. A Carmel service page and reviews from customers there help organic reach, but map rankings cannot be promised. Never offer a discount or gift for a review, since Google prohibits incentivized reviews and can remove them.

### How should event businesses prepare for big Indy weekends?

Update hours ahead of time, post about the event on Google, and keep your website menu or booking page current. AI assistants and Maps both lean on fresh, consistent details. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

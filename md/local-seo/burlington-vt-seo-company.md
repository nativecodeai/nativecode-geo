## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Burlington?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Burlington businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Burlington work. We do not have a Burlington office.

### Can a Burlington business rank in Williston or Essex?

Partly. Map results lean toward the closest options, so a downtown Burlington address has less pull in Williston or Essex. A service area, useful town pages and reviews from customers there can help organic visibility. Map placement is not guaranteed.

### Should Burlington businesses publish French content for Quebec visitors?

If French-speaking visitors are a real share of your customers, a French page for key services or menus can help. Use hreflang tags, keep prices clear, and answer French reviews as well as English ones. Keep your business name, address and phone number identical in every language version so search engines treat them as one business.

### How do winter and summer change Burlington search?

Summer brings lake, waterfront and patio searches, while winter shifts attention to ski trips and indoor spots. Update hours, photos and Business Profile posts as each season starts so listings match what you offer. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Cincinnati?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Cincinnati businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Cincinnati work. We do not have a Cincinnati office.

### Can a Cincinnati business rank in Northern Kentucky?

Yes, for nearby searchers, since Google Maps uses distance more than state lines. Add Covington, Newport and Florence to your service area if you serve them, and build a page for Northern Kentucky with real projects there. Keep any state licensing details clear.

### Should Cincinnati businesses build pages for each suburb?

Only for suburbs you really serve. People in Mason, West Chester or Blue Ash often search with their own city name. A page with local projects and service details gives Google something specific to show, while thin copies of the same page do not help.

### How do Cincinnati B2B firms get cited in AI answers?

AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer buyer questions directly. Publish a page for each service with clear scope, process and proof, and keep your company facts consistent across your site, LinkedIn and profiles. Structured data helps those tools read the page.

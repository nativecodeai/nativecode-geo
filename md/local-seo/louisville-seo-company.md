## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Louisville?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Louisville businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Louisville work. We do not have a Louisville office.

### Can a Louisville business rank in Jeffersonville and New Albany?

It can help to set a service area that includes Southern Indiana and add a page about that work. Map results still favor nearby businesses, so results across the river are not guaranteed. Each area page should name real projects, streets or landmarks there, since near-duplicate pages that only swap the city name rarely rank.

### Should I mention neighborhoods like the Highlands or St. Matthews?

Yes, where you serve them. Locals often search by neighborhood. Pages that describe real work in the Highlands or St. Matthews help searchers and AI tools understand your coverage. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

### How early should I prepare my listing for Derby season?

Update hours, services and photos a few weeks before the crowds arrive. Add Google posts about special menus or packages. Fresh, consistent details help Maps and AI answers stay accurate. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Nashville?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Nashville businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Nashville work. We do not have a Nashville office.

### We are in Nashville but most clients live in Franklin. Can we reach them?

Google weighs distance in map results, so a Davidson County address has limits in Franklin. A service area that includes Williamson County, a useful Franklin page and reviews from customers there help organic visibility. No one can guarantee map placement.

### Should our location pages target neighborhoods like 12 South or Germantown?

Only if you have something specific to say. A neighborhood page should include real projects, directions or details from that area. Thin pages that only swap the neighborhood name tend to get ignored. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### How do tourists find Nashville restaurants and bars through AI tools?

Visitors often ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Google's AI results for places near Broadway or The Gulch. Those answers lean on Business Profile data, reviews and clear menu and hours information on your site. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Boise?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Boise businesses. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Boise work. We do not have a Boise office.

### My office is in Boise. Can I rank in Meridian and Nampa too?

Map results weigh distance, so a Boise address has a harder time in Nampa. Service area settings, suburb pages and reviews that mention those cities help, but no one can promise map rankings there. Google prohibits offering discounts or gifts for reviews, so the safest approach is to ask every customer soon after the job.

### Should my site mention Boise neighborhoods like the North End?

Yes, if you actually serve them. Naming the North End, the Bench or Southeast Boise in real project notes helps searchers and AI tools see where you work. Plain answers to common customer questions on your own site give Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT clear text to quote.

### How many reviews does a Boise business need?

There is no fixed number. What matters is a steady flow of honest reviews and replies over time. Compare your count and recency to the businesses already showing in your Boise map results. A useful area page describes real jobs, travel times or customers in that place rather than repeating the same text.

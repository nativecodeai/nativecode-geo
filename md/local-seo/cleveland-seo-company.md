## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Cleveland?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Cleveland businesses. We meet over video in the same time zone, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Cleveland work. We do not have a Cleveland office.

### Can a Cleveland business rank in the suburbs?

In organic results, yes, with useful pages for suburbs like Lakewood, Westlake or Beachwood that describe your real work there. Google Maps favors nearby businesses, so results depend on distance from your address. A staffed location in a suburb can have its own profile.

### How do Cleveland manufacturers get found online?

Buyers search by capability, material and industry, not only by city. Build a page for each capability with specifications, certifications and photos of real work. Clear, specific pages help Google and AI tools like ChatGPT match you to buyer questions.

### How should Cleveland businesses handle winter weather in search?

Lake-effect snow can change hours and demand quickly. Update your Google Business Profile when you close or change hours, and keep pages for seasonal services like snow removal or furnace repair current before winter starts. Posting updates during storms tells customers and Google you are open.

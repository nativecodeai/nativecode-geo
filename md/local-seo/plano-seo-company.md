## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Plano?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with Plano businesses. We plan over video, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your real photos and job details so your listings show actual Plano work. We do not have an office in Plano.

### Should a Plano business target Plano or Dallas in search?

Target Plano first. People in Collin County often search by their own city name, and Google Maps favors nearby businesses. Build pages for Plano and the suburbs you serve, like Allen or Frisco, and only build a Dallas page if you really do work there.

### How do Plano B2B firms get found by corporate buyers?

Corporate buyers research online before they call. Publish a page for each service with clear scope, process and proof, keep your company facts consistent across the web and add schema. That helps Google and AI tools like ChatGPT describe what you do accurately.

### Is multilingual SEO worth it for Plano businesses?

Often, yes. The Plano area has large Chinese, Indian, Korean and Vietnamese communities. If your staff serve customers in those languages, add pages written by fluent speakers, list the languages on your profile and reply to reviews in the language they were written in.

## How do Minnesota customers search for local businesses?

Minneapolis and St. Paul hold most of the demand, with St. Cloud, Rochester and Duluth as separate markets. Cabin areas like Brainerd and the North Shore add summer searches.

01

### Two downtowns in one metro

Minneapolis and St. Paul sit side by side, but people search them separately. A business near the line should be clear which city it is in and which it serves.

02

### Winter service demand

Snow removal, furnace repair and auto services see searches spike when the cold arrives. Profiles should show winter hours and emergency availability.

03

### Medical visitors in Rochester

Rochester draws patients and families from far away for care at Mayo Clinic. Hotels, dining and transport there serve people who do not know the city.

## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Minnesota?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Minnesota. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Minnesota work. We do not have an office in Minnesota.

### Should I list both Minneapolis and St. Paul if I serve the whole metro?

If you are a service-area business, you can list both. Build a page for each city that reflects real work you do there. A storefront will still rank most strongly near its own address. Review your listings on Apple Maps, Bing Places and the main local directories once a year and fix any old details.

### How should a lake resort handle off-season searches?

Keep your profile open and accurate all year, with special hours for closures. Post updates about winter activities if you offer them. Off-season searches often come from people planning next summer. Local rankings depend on distance, relevance and prominence, so a complete profile, accurate categories and recent reviews all count.

### Do reviews need to mention the town?

You cannot ask for specific wording, but customers often name the town on their own. Asking soon after a visit tends to produce detailed reviews. Never offer anything in exchange for a review. Set special hours in your Google Business Profile ahead of time so Maps and AI answers do not show outdated hours on busy days.

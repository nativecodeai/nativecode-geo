## Local SEO questions

### Does NativeCode work with businesses in Washington?

Yes. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based agency that works remotely with businesses across Washington State. We plan over video calls, work directly in your Google Business Profile and site, and use your own photos and job details so your listings show real Washington State work. We do not have an office in Washington State.

### Should a Seattle company build separate pages for Eastern Washington?

Only if you serve those areas. Spokane and the Tri-Cities are distinct markets with their own competitors. A page with real projects, service details and travel logistics gives Google and AI tools something specific to show. Writing your service details as short, direct answers makes it easier for AI tools to quote you accurately.

### How do Vancouver, WA businesses avoid confusion with Vancouver, BC?

Use Vancouver, WA in titles, schema and citations, and mention Clark County and the Portland metro on your pages. Consistent state naming helps search engines and AI tools place you in the right Vancouver. Check Apple Maps, Bing Places and Yelp at least once a year so old or mismatched details do not linger.

### Do Washington tourism businesses need to update listings by season?

Yes. Ski areas, ferry towns, wineries and park gateways see big seasonal swings. Update hours, photos and posts as each season starts so travelers and AI answers get current details. Write each area page around real work you have done there; pages that only swap the town name rarely help.

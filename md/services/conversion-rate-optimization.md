Conversion rate optimization gets more revenue from the visitors you already have by fixing the pages where they drop off. Questions

## Conversion rate optimization questions

### What does CRO include?

CRO includes product and collection page optimization, A/B testing, checkout friction audits, and fit and sizing tools. The work starts with research into where and why shoppers leave, then moves to page changes that answer their questions, such as sizing, delivery dates and returns.

### Do we need a lot of traffic to run tests?

Split tests need enough traffic to reach a clear result. On smaller stores we use session recordings, surveys and proven fixes instead of split tests, then watch the conversion rate before and after. Larger stores can test several page changes in parallel.

### Which platforms do you work on?

We work on Shopify and Webflow. On Shopify that covers themes, product and collection templates, apps and checkout settings. On Webflow it covers landing pages, forms and CMS templates. We make the changes ourselves, so tests do not wait in a developer queue.

### Will page changes hurt our SEO?

No. We keep indexable copy, schema and internal links in place when we change a page layout, and we check rankings after each release. Conversion and search work best together, so any test that removes content search engines rely on is flagged before it goes live.

### How do you measure success?

We measure conversion rate, revenue per visitor and average order value by page type. Each change is logged with its date, so results can be tied to specific releases. We report revenue impact rather than click rates on a single button.

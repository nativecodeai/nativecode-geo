## What does a schema markup audit check?

A schema markup audit checks these five things in order, because valid markup only helps when it is also accurate.

1. Is the markup valid? JSON-LD syntax, required and recommended properties, and errors in the Rich Results Test.
2. Does it match the page? Prices, availability, ratings and FAQ text must match what visitors see.
3. Are entities connected? One Organization @id, sameAs links to real profiles, and the same name, address and phone everywhere.
4. Is it eligible? Which rich results Google still shows for your types, and which no longer apply.
5. Does it hold after releases? Search Console enhancement reports and spot checks after theme or CMS changes.

## Questions about schema markup

### What is schema markup?

Schema markup is structured data, usually written as JSON-LD, that describes a page using the Schema.org vocabulary shared by the major search engines. It tells Google, Bing and AI answer engines exactly what a page is about, such as a product with a price, a service, an article or a business with an address, instead of leaving them to infer it from the text.

### Does schema markup improve rankings?

Schema markup is not a direct ranking factor, according to Google. It helps search engines understand your pages and can make them eligible for rich results such as product prices, review stars and breadcrumbs, which change how your listing looks in search. Eligibility does not guarantee a rich result: Google decides when to show one.

### Which schema types should my site use?

Most sites need Organization or LocalBusiness, BreadcrumbList on inner pages, and one type per template: Product and Offer for product pages, Service for service pages and Article for posts. FAQPage fits pages with a visible FAQ. Review and AggregateRating belong only on pages that show genuine reviews and follow Google's review snippet guidelines.

### How do you add schema markup on Shopify, Webflow or WordPress?

We add JSON-LD to templates rather than page by page. On Shopify that means Liquid theme sections that pull product, price and availability data. On Webflow we use page and CMS template custom code bound to CMS fields. On WordPress we configure an SEO plugin or write theme code. Every template is validated before release.

### Does structured data help with AI search?

Yes, structured data helps AI answer engines read your entity facts consistently: your business name, services, products and locations. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity still decide what to cite on their own, so schema works alongside clear, crawlable content and consistent facts across the web. We treat it as one input, not a guarantee of citations.

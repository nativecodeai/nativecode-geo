## Partner marketing questions

### What does partner program strategy include?

Partner program strategy includes positioning, partner SEO, asset development and measurement. Assets cover landing pages, co-marketing content and enablement material. The measurement system ties partner activity to pipeline and revenue, so you can see which partners and assets earn their place.

### Is this affiliate marketing?

No. We build durable partner programs with integrations, resellers, agencies and strategic co-marketing relationships, not affiliate link farms. The focus is on partners who share your customers and can sell alongside you over years, not on paying per click for traffic.

### How does this connect to your search work?

Partner programs create demand that lands on pages. We build those pages like everything else we make: structured, measurable and discoverable in search and AI answers. Integration and partner pages often rank for searches that buyers make right before they choose a product.

### Do you work with new or established programs?

Both. New programs get positioning and foundations, such as partner tiers, a partner page and first assets. Established programs get sharper strategy, assets that perform in search, and measurement that holds up with leadership when budgets are reviewed each year.

### How do you measure success?

We measure partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline, asset performance in search and AI answers, and program revenue. Each partner and asset is tracked separately, so you can see which relationships deserve more investment and which need a new plan or a different partner offer.

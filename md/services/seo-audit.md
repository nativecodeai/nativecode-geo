## Questions about SEO audits

### Is the SEO audit really free?

Yes. You get the findings and the priority list whether or not you hire us. We offer the free audit because it is the fastest way for both sides to see whether there is a fit, and because a clear list of fixes is useful to your team either way.

### What do I get from the audit?

You get a written list of the issues holding your rankings back, the pages they affect, the fix for each and an order to do them in, ranked by revenue impact. It covers technical SEO, content, links and AI search visibility.

### How long does the free audit take?

A senior strategist reviews your site by hand, so timing depends on its size and complexity. We confirm when to expect the findings once we receive your request, and we email them as soon as the review is done. A full paid audit for a large or enterprise site is scoped separately.

### What is the difference between the app and the audit?

The NativeCode Audit app at app.nativecode.ai runs an automated check of key technical and AI visibility signals that you can repeat any time. The free audit on this page is a manual review by a senior strategist who explains causes and priorities, which an automated tool cannot do.

### Do you need access to our analytics?

No, not for the free audit. We can review a lot from outside the site, including crawlability, structure, content and AI answers. If you share read access to Search Console and GA4, the findings get more specific, and we treat that data as confidential.

## Interactive tools development questions

### What kinds of tools do you build?

We build calculators, configurators, quizzes, sizing tools and data-driven web apps. They fit anywhere an interactive answer beats a static page for the searches you want to own, such as pricing estimates, fit guides or health and finance calculators with the method shown on the page.

### Have you built any yourselves?

Yes. TDEE.co, BraSize.ai and ElixMD.com are our own products, designed, built and ranked in house. They are live and competing in hard niches. Building our own tools is how we learned what makes one rank, get cited and keep users coming back.

### Why do tools outperform articles?

Tools capture high-intent searches, attract links and citations naturally, keep people engaged and are hard for competitors to copy. An article can be rewritten in an afternoon. A useful calculator with sound logic, clear inputs and fast pages takes real work to match.

### Do you maintain tools after launch?

Yes. Ongoing development, performance monitoring and search updates are available, so the tool keeps compounding instead of decaying. That includes fixing edge cases users report, updating formulas when standards change and adding pages for related searches the tool can answer.

### How do you measure success?

We measure rankings, AI citations, usage and conversion, tied to revenue and demand captured. Usage data shows which inputs people use and where they stop, which guides the next round of improvements to both the tool and the pages around it.

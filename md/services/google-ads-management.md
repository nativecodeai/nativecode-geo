## Questions about Google Ads management

### How much should we spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, keyword costs and how many leads you need. We look at search volume and competitor activity during the review and recommend a starting budget, then adjust based on cost per lead once tracking is in place.

### How fast do Google Ads produce leads?

Campaigns can start producing leads soon after launch, once tracking is working. Results usually improve over the first few months as we collect search term and conversion data, add negative keywords and move budget away from searches that do not convert. We report what changed every week.

### Do you manage Microsoft Ads as well?

Yes. We import and adapt Google Ads campaigns to Microsoft Ads where your buyers search on Bing, Edge and partner sites. Keywords, ads and budgets are adjusted for the platform rather than copied blindly, and we report both platforms together so you can compare cost per lead.

### What is the difference between this and PPC management?

Google Ads management focuses on Google and Microsoft search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns. Our PPC management service runs paid media across Google, Meta and LinkedIn as one program with shared tracking and reporting. Many clients start with Google Ads and add other channels later.

### Will we own the account?

Yes. The Google Ads account, the data and the conversion setup belong to you. We work through access you grant from your own account, and everything stays with you if we stop working together. We never run client spend through accounts we own.

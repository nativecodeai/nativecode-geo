## Questions about web design

### How long does a website redesign take?

A marketing site redesign usually takes a few months from kickoff to launch. The timing depends on page count, content and integrations, and ecommerce or custom builds take longer. You get a written timeline before work starts, and we review progress with you at each stage so there are no surprises at launch.

### Will a redesign hurt our SEO?

It should not, if the redesign is planned for search. We map every existing URL, carry over content and schema, set 301 redirects before launch and check Search Console after release. Most ranking losses after a redesign come from missing redirects, which is why we plan them first.

### Which platform do you recommend?

It depends on who edits the site and what it must do. Webflow suits marketing sites with frequent edits, Shopify suits ecommerce, WordPress suits content-heavy teams already on it, and custom builds suit apps and portals. We recommend a platform after the review, not before.

### Do you write the content too?

Yes, if you need it. We can write page copy built around what your buyers search, or work with your team's drafts and edit them for clarity and SEO. Either way, every page gets a clear H1, structure and meta data before launch.

### Can our team edit the site after launch?

Yes. We build with a CMS and component system your team can edit without a developer, and we hand over a short guide for the main page types. Ongoing support is available if you want us to keep improving the site.

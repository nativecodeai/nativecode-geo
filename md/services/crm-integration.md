## CRM integration questions

### Which CRMs do you integrate with?

We work mainly with HubSpot and Salesforce, and with other CRMs that have a documented API. We connect them to website forms, Shopify and other ecommerce platforms, Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn and email platforms such as Klaviyo. Native integrations come first, with middleware or custom code only where they fall short.

### How do you pass UTM data and click IDs into the CRM?

We capture UTM parameters, click IDs such as GCLID and FBCLID, the landing page and the referrer when a visitor arrives, store them in first-party cookies and write them into hidden form fields. When the form is submitted, the CRM saves them on the contact and the deal. Reports can then show which campaign produced each lead, opportunity and closed deal.

### What are offline conversion imports?

Offline conversion imports send results that happen after the form fill, such as a qualified lead or a closed deal, back to the ad platforms. Google Ads matches them by click ID or enhanced conversions data, and Meta receives them through the Conversions API. Bidding can then optimize toward leads that turn into revenue instead of every form submission.

### Can you sync Shopify customers and orders to HubSpot or Salesforce?

Yes. We sync Shopify customers, orders and products to HubSpot with its Shopify integration or to Salesforce with a connector or middleware, then map fields and lifecycle stages. Sales and service teams see order history on each record, and marketing can segment by purchase behavior. We test duplicates and edge cases such as guest checkouts before switching it on.

### How do you keep CRM data clean?

We keep CRM data clean with rules at the point of entry and regular checks. That means required fields, standard values for sources and stages, deduplication by email and company domain, and naming rules for properties. We also audit the CRM every month for duplicates, empty fields and broken integrations, and fix issues before they reach reports.

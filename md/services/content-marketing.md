## How do you write content that AI answers cite?

AI answer engines quote content that is easy to extract and trust. We write to these rules.

1. Answer the question in the first one or two sentences.
2. Use headings phrased the way people ask.
3. Put steps in numbered lists and comparisons in tables.
4. Name concrete things: platforms, places and standards.
5. Keep facts consistent with the rest of the site and add schema.

## Questions about content marketing

### What does a content marketing agency do?

A content marketing agency plans, writes and publishes content that brings the right audience to your site and moves them toward buying. For us that means SEO content built around buyer questions, structured to rank in Google and to be cited in AI answers.

### Who writes the content?

Our writers and editors produce briefs and drafts, and your subject experts review them for accuracy before anything is published. For technical topics we interview your team first, so each piece reflects real expertise and first-hand detail rather than a summary of articles that already rank.

### How is this different from topic clusters?

Topic clusters are one part of content marketing: a pillar page and linked supporting articles on one subject. Content marketing also covers research, the editorial calendar, writing, refreshes of older posts and measurement across all your topics. Our topic clusters page explains that method in detail.

### How long until content ranks?

New articles often start ranking within a few months, depending on your site's authority and the competition for each topic. Clusters build faster than single posts because the linked articles support each other. We track rankings, AI citations and leads by article every month.

### Do you use AI to write content?

We use AI tools for research and first-draft support, but every piece is planned, edited and fact-checked by people. Content that only repeats what is already online rarely ranks or gets cited, so each article needs original detail from your team and a clear point of view.

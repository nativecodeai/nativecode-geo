## Shopify SEO questions

### What does Shopify architecture include?

Shopify architecture includes collection taxonomy, URL governance, faceted navigation strategy, product schema, internal linking and indexation control. It is the structural layer that decides how search engines read your catalog, which collections rank and which filter pages stay out of the index.

### Can you fix an existing store without replatforming?

Yes. We restructure collections, navigation and schema in place, keep what already ranks and map redirects wherever URLs change. Most stores do not need a new platform to fix search problems. They need a clear plan for which pages exist and why.

### Do you handle product schema?

Yes. We set up full structured data across every SKU, including Product, Offer, Review and Breadcrumb graphs. That qualifies your catalog for rich results and helps AI shopping answers read prices, availability and reviews correctly. Markup always matches what the page shows.

### Does this work with AI answer engines?

Yes. Entity and schema systems make your products easier to cite in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity shopping answers. Accurate product facts, consistent naming and clear buying guides give those tools reliable sources to quote when shoppers ask what to buy.

### How do you measure success?

We measure organic revenue, indexation health, ranking coverage across the catalog and AI citations. These are commerce metrics, not traffic for its own sake. Reports break results down by collection, so you can see which parts of the catalog are growing.

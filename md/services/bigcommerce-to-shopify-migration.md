URLs and redirects

## How do BigCommerce URLs map to Shopify?

BigCommerce URLs map to Shopify's fixed /products/, /collections/, /pages/ and /blogs/ paths, so most URLs change and each needs a 301 redirect. BigCommerce can serve products and categories from the root of the domain; Shopify cannot.

### Product pages

/blue-jacket/ becomes /products/blue-jacket. Older stores that still use .html product URLs are mapped the same way.

### Category and brand pages

/jackets/ becomes /collections/jackets. Brand pages become collections built from the Shopify vendor field.

### Web pages and blog posts

/shipping-returns/ becomes /pages/shipping-returns, and /blog/post-name/ becomes /blogs/news/post-name.

### Filter and search URLs

Filtered category and search URLs redirect to the parent collection, not one by one. Shopify Search & Discovery handles the new filters.

## Questions about BigCommerce to Shopify migration

### Will we lose SEO rankings moving from BigCommerce to Shopify?

Not if the migration is planned for search. Because Shopify adds /products/ and /collections/ to URLs, nearly every BigCommerce product and category URL changes. We map each one with a 301 redirect, carry over titles, meta descriptions and content, and monitor Search Console after launch to fix any gaps quickly.

### How do BigCommerce options and modifiers move to Shopify?

Variant options move to Shopify variants, and modifiers move to line item properties or a product options app. Shopify allows three options per product, so products with more option types are restructured, split into separate products or handled with an options app. We agree the approach for each product type before importing anything.

### What happens to BigCommerce custom fields?

Custom fields become Shopify metafields. We create a metafield definition for each field you use, import the values and show them on product pages through the theme. Fields that shoppers filter by can also power collection filters in Shopify Search & Discovery, so specs stay useful after the move.

### Can you migrate BigCommerce B2B Edition to Shopify?

Yes, usually to Shopify Plus B2B. Company accounts, buyers, customer group pricing and payment terms map to Shopify companies, catalogs and payment terms. Features such as shopping lists, quote workflows or a custom buyer portal may need an app or custom work, so we list each B2B Edition feature you use and plan its replacement first.

### Can customers keep their accounts and order history?

Customer records, addresses and order history can move from BigCommerce to Shopify, but passwords cannot be migrated for security reasons. After launch, customers receive an account invitation so they can set a new password. We import historical orders for reference and reporting and check totals on a staging store first.

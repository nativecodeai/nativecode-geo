Questions

## Email marketing agency questions

### Which platforms do you work with?

We work with Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Constant Contact, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Omnisend and most other email platforms. We log in and build every sequence directly in your account. Klaviyo is our deepest specialty for ecommerce, because its flows and segmentation go further than most tools.

### Can you take over an existing program?

Yes. We start with an audit of your flows, campaigns, lists and deliverability, keep what works and rebuild the rest in phases so revenue is never put at risk. Most takeovers begin with the highest-value sequences, such as welcome, cart and post-purchase, before campaigns are changed.

### How do you measure success?

We measure revenue per recipient, sequence revenue, list growth and deliverability. Reports come monthly alongside your other channels, so email is judged on the same revenue terms as paid and organic search, not on open rates that privacy features now distort.

### Do you run SMS as well as email?

Yes. We build SMS alongside email in platforms such as Klaviyo, with consent capture, quiet hours and separate flows for subscribers who opt in. SMS works best for time-sensitive messages like shipping and launches, so we plan both channels together to avoid sending the same message twice.

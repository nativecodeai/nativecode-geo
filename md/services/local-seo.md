## Local SEO services questions

### What is local AEO?

Local AEO is answer engine optimization for local questions. It means making your business facts, services, areas and reviews clear and consistent so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Mode can recommend you when someone asks for a business nearby. It builds on the same profile, page and review work as local SEO.

### How long does local SEO take?

Local SEO usually shows early movement within weeks and builds over several months. Profile, category and citation fixes can move map rankings quickly. Location pages, reviews and local links take longer to compound. We report progress monthly, by location and search term, so you can see what is moving and what comes next.

### Do I need an office in each town to rank there?

No, but distance matters for map results. Google weighs the distance from the searcher to your verified address, so map rankings are strongest near your location. Service area businesses can still rank in nearby towns with strong relevance and reviews, and a useful page for each area helps in regular results.

### Do you work with multi-location brands?

Yes. We manage Google Business Profiles, location pages and reviews across many locations from one plan, with reporting by location so you can compare markets side by side. Franchises, chains and clinics with several branches usually start with a data cleanup across every profile before new pages are built.

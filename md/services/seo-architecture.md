# SEO architecture that scales with the business.

SEO architecture is the structure of a site: its taxonomy, URL hierarchy, internal links and indexation rules, which decide how search engines and AI answer engines read it. NativeCode is an Atlanta-based team that designs SEO architecture for growing and enterprise sites nationwide, before content scales, so you do not pay for rework later.

## What does SEO architecture cover?

SEO architecture covers the structure under every page: taxonomy, URL hierarchy, internal link graphs, indexation rules, entities and schema. It is delivered as a blueprint your product and engineering teams can build from, or implemented by us end to end.

URL mapping, redirects and launch QA, so rankings survive the move.

SEO architecture should be planned upstream, inside your roadmap, before pages and templates are built. Fixing structure after launch costs more than designing it first.

## SEO architecture questions

### Is this SEO or something broader?

Both. Structure, entities and schema decide how Google and AI answer engines read a site, so we design for them as one system rather than chasing rankings page by page. The same architecture supports navigation, analytics and content planning, not only search.

### How do you work with product and engineering?

We join planning, write specs your engineers can build from and check each release before it ships. Decisions made upstream then do not create SEO problems downstream. Specs include URL rules, template requirements, schema and acceptance checks your team can test against.

### Do you only advise, or do you implement?

Both. Engagements range from an audit and roadmap to a full implementation build or an enterprise migration, as a one-off project or a monthly governance retainer. Many teams start with the blueprint and bring us in to implement the parts their engineers cannot schedule.

### Do you cover AI search visibility?

Yes. We look at how large language models and answer engines interpret your content, entities and structure. Then we build the structured data and page formats that make citation more likely, and track how Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity describe the site.

### How do you measure success?

We measure crawl efficiency, indexation health, ranking coverage, demand captured and revenue from organic search. Every recommendation is tied to one of them, so you can see which structural changes paid off and which areas of the site still need work next quarter.

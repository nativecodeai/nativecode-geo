## What can you export from Wix, and what must be rebuilt?

Wix can export product, contact and order data, but your design, apps and most page content have to be rebuilt in Shopify. Here is how each part moves.

### Products and variants

Wix Stores products export to CSV. We reshape options, variants and images to fit Shopify's product structure and check every SKU before import.

### Customers and orders

Customer records and order history move with a migration app or the Shopify API. Passwords cannot be migrated, so customers get an invitation to reactivate their account.

### Blog posts and pages

Wix blog posts at /post/ become Shopify articles under /blogs/, and pages move to /pages/. Titles, meta descriptions and image alt text come across with them.

## Questions about Wix to Shopify migration

### Will we lose SEO rankings moving from Wix to Shopify?

Not if the migration is planned for search. Wix and Shopify use different URL structures, so a product at /product-page/name moves to /products/name on Shopify. We map every old URL to its new one with a 301 redirect, carry over titles and meta descriptions, and watch Search Console after launch to catch gaps.

### Why do stores move from Wix to Shopify?

Most stores move when they outgrow Wix as a commerce platform. Common reasons are a growing catalog with more variants, inventory across several locations, integrations with ERP, 3PL or marketplace systems, and the need for apps Wix does not offer. Shopify is built around commerce, with a large App Store and an API for custom work.

### Can you move customer accounts and order history from Wix?

Yes. Customer records and order history can be moved to Shopify with a migration app or the Shopify API. Passwords cannot be migrated for security reasons, so customers are invited to reactivate their accounts with an account activation email. We test the data on a staging store first so customer and order records match before launch.

### Can we keep our Wix design on Shopify?

Not directly. Wix designs cannot be moved to Shopify, so the design is rebuilt as a Shopify theme. We can match your current look closely or use the move to improve it, with templates built for your products, collections and content. The theme is built on staging and reviewed before launch.

### How long does a Wix to Shopify migration take?

It depends on the store. Catalog size, data quality, the number of Wix apps and features to replace and the amount of design work all affect timing. Small stores move faster than large catalogs with custom features. You get a written timeline before work starts, and launch is planned for a low-traffic window.

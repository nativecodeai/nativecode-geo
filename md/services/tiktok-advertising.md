## What makes TikTok ad creative work?

TikTok ad creative works when it looks native to the feed and hooks viewers in the first seconds. These are the rules we build and test to.

1. Open with the hook in the first two seconds: the problem or the product in use.
2. Shoot vertical, full screen and native to TikTok, not resized TV or Meta ads.
3. Use captions, on-screen text and sound that suit the format and the voiceover.
4. Test one variable at a time on a set weekly cadence: hook, offer, creator or format.
5. Refresh creative before frequency climbs and results fade.

## Questions about TikTok ads

### Is TikTok advertising worth it for our business?

TikTok advertising is worth testing when your buyers use TikTok and you can supply or produce regular short-form video. It suits consumer brands, ecommerce and many service businesses. We start with a free account review or a small, tracked test, so the decision rests on your own sales or lead data rather than platform averages.

### What are Spark Ads on TikTok?

Spark Ads are TikTok ads that run from existing organic posts, either your own account's or a creator's with their permission. The ad keeps the original handle, likes and comments, so it looks native to the feed. We manage the authorization codes, briefs and usage terms, and test Spark Ads against standard in-feed ads.

### Why does the TikTok Events API matter?

The Events API sends conversions from your server or platform, such as Shopify, directly to TikTok, so fewer purchases and leads are lost to browser privacy settings and ad blockers. Paired with the TikTok Pixel and deduplicated, it gives TikTok more complete data, which improves reporting and helps its delivery system find buyers.

### How much should we spend on TikTok ads?

It depends on your goals, order or lead value, and how many creative tests you want to run. After the review we recommend a starting daily budget that gives each test enough data to read clearly. Then we pace spend toward the campaigns and creative that produce sales or leads and cut the rest.

### How does TikTok fit with Meta and Google Ads?

TikTok usually works as a discovery channel that creates demand, while Google Ads captures people already searching and Meta retargets and scales what works. We plan the three together, reuse creative between TikTok and Meta where it fits, and read results in GA4 and your store or CRM so each channel gets fair credit.

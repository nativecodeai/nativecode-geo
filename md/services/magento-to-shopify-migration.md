## How do Magento URLs map to Shopify?

Magento URLs map to Shopify's fixed /products/, /collections/, /pages/ and /blogs/ paths, so almost every URL changes and needs a 301 redirect. Magento lets you set any URL key and suffix; Shopify does not.

### Product pages

/blue-jacket.html or /men/jackets/blue-jacket.html becomes /products/blue-jacket. Each category path version of a product URL gets its own redirect.

### Category pages

/men/jackets.html becomes /collections/mens-jackets. Shopify collections are flat, so nested categories need a menu and filter plan.

### CMS pages and blog posts

/about-us becomes /pages/about-us. Posts from a Magento blog extension move to Shopify blogs at /blogs/news/post-handle.

### Store views and filters

Store view URLs for other countries or languages map to Shopify Markets. Layered navigation filter URLs redirect to the parent collection, not one by one.

## Questions about Magento to Shopify migration

### Will we lose SEO rankings moving from Magento to Shopify?

Not if the migration is planned for search. Most ranking losses come from missing redirects, and a Magento store can have thousands of URL rewrites, including .html suffixes and category paths. We map each one to its Shopify URL, carry over titles, meta descriptions and content, and monitor Search Console after launch to catch gaps early.

### Can you migrate Adobe Commerce and Magento 1 stores?

Yes. We migrate Magento 2, Adobe Commerce and Adobe Commerce Cloud stores, and Magento 1 stores still running after Magento 1 reached end of life in June 2020. The data model is similar across versions, so the work is mapping products, attributes, customers and URLs to Shopify, then replacing extensions and integrations.

### What happens to configurable products and custom attributes?

Configurable products become Shopify products with variants, and each child simple product becomes a variant with its own SKU, price and inventory. Shopify allows three options per product, so products with more option attributes need a different structure or an options app. Custom attributes used for specs or filters move to metafields.

### Can customers keep their accounts and order history?

Customer records, addresses and order history can move from Magento to Shopify, but passwords cannot be migrated for security reasons. After launch, customers receive an account invitation so they can set a new password. We import historical orders for reference and reporting and check totals on a staging store first.

### Does Shopify support Magento B2B features and store views?

Largely, through Shopify Plus. Shopify Plus B2B covers company accounts, catalogs with custom pricing, payment terms and quantity rules, which replaces many Adobe Commerce B2B setups. Store views for other countries or languages usually map to Shopify Markets. Features such as requisition lists or complex quote flows may need an app or custom work.

### What happens to our ERP and EDI integrations?

We rebuild them for Shopify before launch. Common ERPs and EDI providers have Shopify connectors, and others can use the Shopify Admin API. We test product, inventory and order flows against the staging store, plan switch-over day so orders keep flowing to your ERP and suppliers, and watch the feeds closely in the weeks after.

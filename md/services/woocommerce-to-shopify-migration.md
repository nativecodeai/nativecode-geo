## How do WooCommerce URLs map to Shopify?

WooCommerce URLs map to Shopify's fixed /products/, /collections/, /pages/ and /blogs/ paths, so product, category and post URLs change and each needs a 301 redirect. Shopify cannot keep WordPress permalink structures.

### Product pages

/product/blue-jacket/ becomes /products/blue-jacket. Variation URLs with attribute parameters redirect to the parent product.

### Category and tag pages

/product-category/jackets/ becomes /collections/jackets. Nested categories become collections linked through menus, and /product-tag/ pages redirect to the closest collection.

### Pages and blog posts

/about/ becomes /pages/about, and a post at /post-name/ becomes /blogs/news/post-name if the blog moves to Shopify.

## Questions about WooCommerce to Shopify migration

### Will we lose SEO rankings moving from WooCommerce to Shopify?

Not if the migration is planned for search. Shopify adds fixed prefixes such as /products/ and /collections/, so most WooCommerce URLs change. We map every product, category, page and post URL with a 301 redirect, carry over SEO titles and meta descriptions from Yoast or Rank Math, and monitor Search Console after launch.

### Should we keep our WordPress blog or move it to Shopify?

It depends on how much the blog does for you. A small blog usually moves cleanly into Shopify blogs with redirects. A large content library that relies on WordPress categories, custom post types or plugins can stay on WordPress, usually on a subdomain. We compare both options against your traffic and publishing needs before deciding.

### What happens to our WooCommerce plugins?

Each plugin is replaced by a native Shopify feature, a Shopify app or custom theme code, and some are no longer needed. Yoast or Rank Math settings move into Shopify SEO fields, product add-ons move to an options app, and Advanced Custom Fields data moves to metafields. We list every active plugin first so nothing is missed.

### Can you migrate WooCommerce Subscriptions?

Yes, to a Shopify subscription app such as Shopify Subscriptions or Recharge. Subscription records, plans and renewal dates can be moved. Whether stored payment methods transfer depends on your payment gateway, so some subscribers may need to re-enter card details. We confirm this with the gateway and the app before launch.

### Can customers keep their accounts and order history?

Customer records, addresses and order history can move from WooCommerce to Shopify, but passwords cannot be migrated for security reasons. After launch, customers receive an account invitation so they can set a new password. We import historical orders for reference and reporting and check totals on a staging store first.

### Why move from WooCommerce to Shopify?

Many stores move to cut maintenance. WooCommerce runs on WordPress, so you manage hosting, security, PHP versions and plugin updates yourself. Shopify is hosted, with security, checkout and platform updates handled by Shopify, so your team works on the store instead of the server. The trade-off is less control over URL structure and server-level code.

## PPC management questions

### Which platforms do you run?

We run Google Ads, Meta Ads and LinkedIn Ads, plus Microsoft Ads where it fits. We pick the mix based on where your buyers are and what each channel costs to reach them, and we move budget between channels as results come in rather than locking it in at the start.

### Do you set up tracking?

Yes. We install or audit the Meta Pixel, Conversions API, GA4 events and UTM tagging before any budget goes live. Where possible we connect results to your CRM or store data, so campaigns are judged on qualified leads and sales rather than clicks.

### How often do you report?

We report weekly. Each report covers spend, results, cost per result and the changes we made that week, with a monthly summary that compares channels side by side, so you can see which campaigns to scale, which to pause and why each change was made.

### Can paid media run alongside our SEO program?

Yes. Running both lets us use search query data to choose ad audiences and keywords, and use ad results to decide which content to build next. Paid search can cover terms while organic pages are still climbing, then step back once rankings hold.

### How do you measure success?

We measure cost per qualified lead or cost per sale, tied to your CRM or store data. Clicks and impressions are tracked, but they are not the goal. Targets are agreed before launch, and every report shows progress against them.

## Questions about SEO consulting

### What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant diagnoses what is limiting organic growth, sets priorities and guides the people who do the work. That includes audits, roadmaps, migration plans, developer specs and release checks. The consultant does not replace your team; they make its search decisions faster and safer.

### Is a consultant better than an SEO agency?

Neither is better in general. A consultant suits teams that already have writers and developers and need senior direction. An agency suits teams that also need the work implemented. We offer both, and many clients start with consulting before adding implementation.

### Who will we work with?

You work directly with a senior strategist, not an account manager. The same person runs the audit, writes the roadmap, joins your calls and reviews the work your team ships. There are no junior handoffs between the sale and the work.

### Can you help with a site migration?

Yes. Migrations are one of the most common consulting projects we take on. We map every URL, plan redirects, review the staging site, check launch day and monitor Search Console afterward. The goal is for rankings and traffic to carry over to the new platform without a long recovery period.

### Do you consult on AI search?

Yes. We advise teams on how to get cited in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity. That covers entity clarity, schema, answer-first content and consistent facts about your organization across your site and the wider web. We also show where AI answers currently name competitors instead of you, and why.

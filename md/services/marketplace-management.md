## Marketplace management questions

### Which marketplaces do you manage?

We manage Amazon, Walmart Marketplace and eBay for brands that also sell through their own store. Each marketplace has its own listing rules, fees, account health metrics and ad platform, so we run them as separate channels that share one catalog and one inventory source. Other marketplaces can be added when they fit your products.

### Can you sync our Shopify catalog and inventory to Amazon and Walmart?

Yes. We connect Shopify or your ERP to each marketplace through native integrations, feed tools or custom connections, then map products, variants and attributes to each marketplace's categories. Inventory and orders sync both ways, so stock sold on one channel updates the others and orders reach your fulfillment team or 3PL without manual entry.

### What is the Amazon Buy Box and why does it matter?

The Buy Box is the Add to Cart section on an Amazon product page, and the seller who holds it gets the sale when a shopper clicks Add to Cart. Amazon awards it based on factors such as price, delivery speed, stock and seller performance. We monitor who holds it, fix the issues we can control and set pricing rules that protect your margin.

### Do you run Amazon and Walmart advertising?

Yes. We run Amazon Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, Walmart Connect and eBay Promoted Listings. Campaigns are built around your product margins, with separate structures for brand, competitor and category searches. We report advertising cost of sales alongside total sales by channel, so ad spend is judged on profit, not only on ad-attributed revenue.

### How does EDI fit into marketplace management?

EDI moves purchase orders, ship notices, inventory and invoices between you, your suppliers and some retail partners in standard formats. When products sold on Amazon, Walmart or eBay ship from a supplier or 3PL, EDI or API connections keep stock and tracking accurate on every channel. Our EDI integration service builds and monitors those connections.

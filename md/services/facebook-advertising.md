## What makes Meta ad creative work?

Creative decides most Meta ad results. These are the rules we build to.

1. Lead with the product or the problem in the first second.
2. Design for sound off, with captions and clear on-screen text.
3. Make vertical versions for Reels and Stories, not just crops.
4. Test one change at a time: hook, offer or format.
5. Refresh creative before results fade, not after.

## Questions about Meta ads

### How much should we spend on Meta ads?

It depends on your goals, average order value or lead value, and how quickly you want to test. After the review we recommend a starting budget that gives each test enough data to read clearly. Then we scale the campaigns and creative that produce results and cut the rest.

### Do you make the ad creative?

Yes. We produce ad creative, product imagery and short-form video in house, and test it against live spend. You can also supply your own photos and video, and we will adapt them for each placement, such as Reels, Stories and feed, so every ad fits the screen it runs on.

### Why does the Conversions API matter?

Browser tracking misses some conversions because of privacy settings and ad blockers. The Conversions API sends events from your server or platform, such as Shopify, directly to Meta. Meta then sees more of your real purchases and leads, which improves reporting and helps its delivery system find buyers.

### Do you run Instagram ads and Facebook ads separately?

They run from the same Meta Ads Manager account. We plan placements so creative fits each surface, such as Instagram Reels, Stories and the Facebook feed. Results are reported by placement where it helps decisions, so you can see whether Instagram or Facebook is bringing better customers.

### Can you work with our Shopify and Klaviyo setup?

Yes. We connect Meta to Shopify for the product catalog and purchase events, and coordinate with Klaviyo so ads and email work together. For example, recent email buyers can be excluded from prospecting ads, and Klaviyo segments can feed custom audiences for retargeting.

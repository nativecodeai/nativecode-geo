## Video production and ad creative questions

### Do you use AI in production?

Yes. We use AI tools for concepting, imagery and video alongside traditional production, and every piece is reviewed and edited by our team before it runs. AI speeds up variations and first drafts. People decide what is on brand, accurate and worth testing.

### Which channels do you produce for?

We produce for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, display and your website. Each placement gets the sizes, lengths and safe zones it needs, such as vertical cuts for Reels and TikTok and longer edits for YouTube and product pages, so one shoot or concept covers every channel you buy.

### How do you decide what works?

Live results decide. Every version is tagged and tested against live spend, and we compare hooks, offers and formats on cost per result rather than taste. The winners shape the next brief, and the losing versions tell us what to stop making.

### How much creative do we need each month?

Enough to keep testing. Most paid social accounts need fresh concepts on a steady schedule because audiences tire of the same ads. We size the volume to your spend and channels after reviewing your account, and the free review on this page is the place to start.

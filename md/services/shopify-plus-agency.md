## Questions about Shopify Plus

### When does a brand need Shopify Plus?

A brand usually needs Shopify Plus when it needs what standard plans limit or leave out: the full B2B feature set, checkout customization across every step, custom apps built on Shopify Functions, expansion stores, Launchpad, or higher API limits for ERP and 3PL integrations. Sales volume alone is not the reason. We check your requirements during the free store review and tell you if a standard plan still fits.

### What replaced checkout.liquid on Shopify Plus?

Checkout Extensibility replaced checkout.liquid. Shopify has deprecated checkout.liquid, so checkout customizations now use checkout UI extensions, Shopify Functions for discount, shipping and payment logic, checkout branding settings and web pixels for tracking. We audit what your current checkout does, rebuild each customization in the supported model and test it in a development store before switching over.

### Can Shopify Plus handle B2B and wholesale?

Yes. Shopify Plus includes B2B features: company accounts with multiple buyers and locations, catalogs and price lists, payment terms, quantity rules and draft orders. Wholesale buyers can order from the same store as retail customers or from a separate expansion store. We set up the B2B configuration and connect it to your ERP so pricing and orders stay in sync.

### Can you migrate our store to Shopify Plus?

Yes. We migrate stores from Magento, BigCommerce, WooCommerce and Salesforce Commerce Cloud, and upgrade standard Shopify stores to Plus. Products, customers, orders and content move over, every URL gets a 301 redirect and SEO elements carry across, all planned to protect search traffic. Our Magento to Shopify migration page covers the process in detail.

### Do you connect Shopify Plus to ERP, 3PL and EDI systems?

Yes. We connect Shopify Plus to ERPs such as NetSuite, to 3PL warehouses and to retail partners over EDI, so orders, inventory, shipments and invoices move without manual entry. We use the Shopify Admin API, webhooks and middleware where it fits, and we monitor the connections after launch so failed syncs are caught and fixed quickly.

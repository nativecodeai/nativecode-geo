Questions

## Webflow development questions

### Do you design and build, or just optimize?

Both. We design and develop complete Webflow sites, and we rebuild or restructure existing ones. Either way, search architecture is part of the build, not a retrofit. That means URL structure, CMS fields, headings and schema are planned before pages are designed.

### Can you work with our existing Webflow site?

Yes. We audit the current structure, then restructure CMS collections, components and schema without breaking what already ranks. URL mapping and redirects are included, and we check Search Console after each release to confirm nothing important dropped out of the index.

### Is this template work or custom?

Custom. Every build is designed around your brand, content model and search strategy. This site and our own products, like TDEE.co and BraSize.ai, are built the same way, with a component system your team can reuse for new pages without a developer.

### Do you handle migrations to Webflow?

Yes, with full URL mapping, redirects and schema preservation so rankings survive the move. Migration is where most SEO damage happens, and planning prevents it. We move content, metadata and structured data, then compare rankings and traffic before and after launch.

### How do you measure success?

We measure crawl efficiency, indexation health, Core Web Vitals, rankings, AI citations and conversion rate on the pages that matter. Each launch is benchmarked against the old site, so you can see what the rebuild changed in search and in leads.

## What makes LinkedIn ads work for B2B?

LinkedIn ads work for B2B when a specific offer reaches the right roles at the right accounts. These are the rules we build and test to.

1. Target by role and account first, then widen only when lead quality holds.
2. Lead with a useful offer, such as a guide, demo or consultation, matched to buying stage.
3. Use Document Ads and Lead Gen Forms to cut friction for busy buyers.
4. Test one variable at a time: offer, headline, format or audience.
5. Judge results by qualified pipeline, not cost per lead alone.

## Questions about LinkedIn ads

### Is LinkedIn advertising worth it for B2B?

LinkedIn advertising is often worth it for B2B when your deal value can support higher click costs, because it lets you target by job title, company and seniority. It suits SaaS, professional services and companies that sell to specific industries. We test with tracked campaigns, so the decision rests on your own pipeline data.

### What is account-based marketing on LinkedIn?

Account-based marketing on LinkedIn means showing ads only to people at a defined list of target companies. We upload company or contact lists as matched audiences, layer job titles and seniority on top, and tailor offers by account tier. Your sales team can then see which target accounts are engaging before they reach out.

### Do LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms sync with our CRM?

Yes. LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms can sync to HubSpot, Salesforce and other CRMs through native integrations or tools such as Zapier. We map fields, add hidden campaign values and test the flow, so every lead reaches your CRM with its source and sales can follow up while interest is fresh.

### How do you set LinkedIn ad budgets and bids?

We set budgets and bids by objective, audience size and lead value. New campaigns often start on maximum delivery bidding to gather data, then move to cost caps or manual bids once we know what a qualified lead is worth. Budget shifts each week toward the audiences and offers that create pipeline.

### How do you report LinkedIn ads to pipeline?

We report LinkedIn ads to pipeline by tying each lead to its campaign and following it through your CRM deal stages. The Insight Tag, Conversions API and CRM sync carry the source forward. Weekly reports show spend, leads, qualified opportunities and pipeline by campaign, so bids and budget follow what produces sales conversations.

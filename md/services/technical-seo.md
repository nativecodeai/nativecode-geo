Technical SEO audit

## What does a technical SEO audit check?

A technical SEO audit checks these areas in order, because each one depends on the one before.

1. Can search engines crawl the site? Robots rules, server responses and internal links.
2. Are the right pages indexed? Canonicals, noindex rules, sitemaps and duplicates.
3. Do pages render and load fast? JavaScript rendering, Core Web Vitals and mobile layout.
4. Is the structure clear? URLs, navigation, breadcrumbs and internal linking depth.
5. Is the content understood? Titles, headings, schema and entity signals for AI search.

## Questions about technical SEO

### What is technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the work that lets search engines and AI crawlers find, render and understand a website. It covers crawling, indexation, site speed, structured data, site architecture and redirects. Content and links only pay off when this foundation is working.

### How is technical SEO different from on-page SEO?

Technical SEO deals with how the whole site is built and served, such as crawl rules, speed and schema. On-page SEO deals with what each page says, such as titles, headings and copy. Most sites need both, and we handle them together.

### Do you implement fixes or only recommend them?

Both. We implement fixes directly on Shopify, Webflow and WordPress, and write developer-ready specs for custom stacks and headless builds. After each release we check Search Console and recrawl the affected pages to confirm the fix worked, then move to the next item on the list.

### How long does a technical SEO audit take?

A technical SEO audit usually takes a few weeks. The timing depends on site size and on how many templates, platforms and markets are involved, so large ecommerce and enterprise sites take longer. You get findings ranked by impact with the pages each issue affects, not a raw export from a crawler.

### Does technical SEO help with AI search?

Yes. AI answer engines rely on crawlable pages, clear structure and structured data to understand what a business does. Clean schema, consistent entity facts and fast, renderable pages make it easier for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity to cite you.

## Questions about Shopify development

### How much does Shopify development cost?

It depends on the number of templates, the apps and integrations involved and whether you are migrating from another platform. After a free store review you get a written scope, timeline and price before any work starts, so you know exactly what is included and what it costs.

### Can you migrate us to Shopify without losing rankings?

Yes. We map every URL on the old store, carry over content, titles and schema, and set 301 redirects before launch. After go-live we watch Search Console and fix any gaps quickly. Our Shopify migration page covers the full process for Magento, BigCommerce, WooCommerce and Wix stores.

### Can you connect our suppliers?

Yes. We have built supplier EDI connections for inventory and order routing for ecommerce brands. Stock levels, purchase orders and shipping confirmations move between Shopify and your suppliers automatically, so your team stops copying data between spreadsheets and customers see accurate stock.

### Do you work on Shopify Plus?

Yes. We build and maintain stores on Shopify and Shopify Plus, including checkout extensions, Shopify Functions, B2B features and multi-store setups for different markets. If you are deciding whether Plus is worth it, we can review your needs during the free store review and give a straight answer.

### Will our team be able to edit the store?

Yes. We build sections and templates your team can edit in the Shopify theme editor without a developer, and we hand over a short guide for the main page types. If you want ongoing help after launch, we can keep improving the store on a monthly basis.

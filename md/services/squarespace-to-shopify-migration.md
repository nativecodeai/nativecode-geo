## What can you export from Squarespace, and what must be rebuilt?

Squarespace can export product, customer and order data, but your design and most page layouts have to be rebuilt in Shopify. Here is how each part moves.

### Products and variants

Squarespace Commerce products export to CSV, though not every product type or field comes across. We reshape variants, options and images to fit Shopify and check every SKU.

### Customers and orders

Customer records and order history move with a migration app or the Shopify API. Passwords cannot be migrated, so customers get an invitation to reactivate their account.

### Blog posts and pages

Squarespace's site export covers blog posts and basic pages, not products or design. Posts become Shopify articles under /blogs/, and pages move to /pages/.

[Shopify SEO →](https://www.nativecode.ai/services/shopify-seo)

### Store and product URLs

Squarespace product URLs sit under the store page, often as /shop/p/product-name. Each one redirects to its Shopify /products/ URL, and store and category pages to /collections/.

## Questions about Squarespace to Shopify migration

### Will we lose SEO rankings moving from Squarespace to Shopify?

Not if the migration is planned for search. Squarespace and Shopify use different URL structures, so a product at /shop/p/name moves to /products/name on Shopify. We map every old URL to its new one with a 301 redirect, carry over titles and meta descriptions, and watch Search Console after launch to catch gaps.

### Why do stores move from Squarespace to Shopify?

Most stores move when they outgrow Squarespace as a commerce platform. Common reasons are a growing catalog with more variants, inventory across several locations, integrations with ERP, 3PL or marketplace systems, and the need for apps Squarespace does not offer. Shopify is built around commerce, with a large App Store and an API for custom work.

### Can you move customer accounts and order history from Squarespace?

Yes. Customer records and order history can be moved to Shopify with a migration app or the Shopify API. Passwords cannot be migrated for security reasons, so customers are invited to reactivate their accounts with an account activation email. We test the data on a staging store first so customer and order records match before launch.

### Can we keep our Squarespace design on Shopify?

Not directly. Squarespace templates cannot be moved to Shopify, so the design is rebuilt as a Shopify theme. We can match your current look closely or use the move to improve it, with templates built for your products, collections and content. The theme is built on staging and reviewed before launch.

### How long does a Squarespace to Shopify migration take?

It depends on the store. Catalog size, data quality, the number of Squarespace features and extensions to replace and the amount of design work all affect timing. Small stores move faster than large catalogs with custom features. You get a written timeline before work starts, and launch is planned for a low-traffic window.

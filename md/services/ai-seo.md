## AI search optimization questions

### What is Answer Engine Optimization?

AEO structures your site and content so AI answer engines like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity can read, trust and cite it. It covers entity optimization, structured data and answer-formatted content. The goal is to be the source an AI answer quotes, not only a link in a list of results.

### How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO earns rankings in result lists. AEO earns citations inside answers. They share foundations like crawlability, structure and authority, but AEO adds entity clarity, machine-readable trust and quotable formatting. Most sites need both, and we run them as one program.

### Can you prove this works?

Yes. Our method holds a verified unbranded AI Overview citation on a DA 34 domain, earned with architecture and content engineering rather than brand weight or link spend. Request the free AI search audit on this page to see how engines read your own site today.

### Do you work alongside existing SEO teams?

Yes. We add AEO systems to existing SEO programs and work with in-house, product and engineering teams without disrupting what already ranks. Most engagements start with an audit your team can act on, followed by schema, entity and content work we ship together.

### How do you measure AI visibility?

We track citations across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity for a fixed set of prompts, plus entity and schema validation. Those sit alongside rankings, demand captured and revenue, so AI visibility is reported on the same terms as the rest of search.

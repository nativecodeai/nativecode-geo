## How do ChatGPT and Perplexity choose which sources to cite?

ChatGPT search and Perplexity run live web searches, read the pages they retrieve and cite the sources that answer most clearly. ChatGPT search relies on search providers including Bing plus OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot crawler, and Perplexity uses PerplexityBot. Pages those systems can crawl, index and quote have the best chance.

01

### Crawler and index access

Rules in robots.txt that allow OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, Bing Webmaster Tools indexing, and pages that render without heavy JavaScript.

02

### Entity clarity

One consistent set of brand facts: your name, what you do, who you serve, locations and products, stated the same way on your site and profiles.

03

### Answer-first content

Pages that answer the question in the first sentence, then back it up with lists, tables and short definitions that are easy to extract.

04

### Third-party mentions

Coverage, reviews, directories and expert sources that answer engines already cite, so your brand appears in the pages they read.

05

### Structured data

Organization, Service, Product and FAQPage JSON-LD that states your facts in a form machines read reliably.

06

### Mention and citation tracking

A fixed prompt set run on a schedule in ChatGPT and Perplexity, logging where you are mentioned, cited or missing.

## ChatGPT SEO questions

### What is ChatGPT SEO?

ChatGPT SEO is the practice of making a brand easy for ChatGPT search to find, understand and cite. It combines crawler access, consistent entity facts, answer-first content, structured data and mentions on trusted third-party sites. The same work usually helps in Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, because these engines read the web in similar ways.

### How does ChatGPT search find sources?

ChatGPT search sends queries to third-party search providers, including Microsoft Bing, and uses OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot crawler to read pages. It then summarizes what it finds and links the sources it relied on. Pages blocked in robots.txt, missing from Bing's index or hard to render are less likely to be read or cited.

### How do you optimize for Perplexity as well?

Perplexity works in a similar way: it runs a live search, reads sources with its PerplexityBot crawler and cites them inline. We allow PerplexityBot in robots.txt, keep key facts in plain text near the top of each page, and pursue mentions on sources Perplexity already cites for your topics. Both engines are tracked with the same prompt set.

### Can you guarantee ChatGPT will cite my brand?

No. Nobody can guarantee a citation in ChatGPT or Perplexity. Answers change with the prompt, the user's location, model updates and whatever the live search returns that day. What we can control is whether your pages are crawlable, clear, consistent and worth citing, and we measure progress on a fixed prompt set instead of promising placements.

### How do you measure ChatGPT visibility?

We run a fixed set of real buyer prompts in ChatGPT and Perplexity on a set schedule and log whether your brand is mentioned, cited or missing, and who appears instead. We pair that with referral traffic from chatgpt.com and perplexity.ai in GA4. Using the same prompts every time keeps the results comparable.

## Link building services questions

### Do you buy links?

No. We do not use paid link networks, PBNs or link exchanges. Those links can be discounted or penalized by Google, and they rarely come from sites your buyers read. Every link we report was earned through outreach, digital PR or an asset that another site chose to cite.

### Where do the links come from?

The links come from news sites, trade publications and niche blogs that cover your category. We earn them through outreach and digital PR, usually built around original data, expert commentary or a useful tool. Relevance to your market matters more to us than raw domain metrics.

### Do links help with AI search?

Yes. AI answer engines lean on sources that other trusted sites cite, so earned coverage supports both rankings and citations. A brand that is mentioned and linked by credible publications is easier for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity to describe accurately.

### How do you report results?

Every placement is listed with the linking URL, the page it points to and the date it went live. Reports also show which target pages gained links and how their rankings moved, so you can connect the coverage to search results instead of counting links on their own.

### Can tools and calculators earn links?

Yes. People link to calculators and tools because they use them, and a good tool keeps earning links long after launch. We build those too, through our interactive tools work, and run outreach to the sites and writers who cover the problem the tool solves.

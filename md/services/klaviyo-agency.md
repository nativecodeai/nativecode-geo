## Klaviyo agency questions

### Which Klaviyo flows should an ecommerce store set up first?

Start with welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment and post-purchase flows, because they reach shoppers at the moments they are most likely to buy. Once those run cleanly, add winback, back in stock and replenishment flows. We build each flow with filters so first-time buyers and repeat customers get different messages, and we test timing before adding more steps.

### Can you take over an existing Klaviyo account?

Yes. We start with an audit of your flows, segments, campaigns, signup forms and deliverability, keep what works and rebuild the rest in phases. Most takeovers begin with data and sending domain fixes, then the highest-value flows, so the program keeps sending while we improve it. You keep full ownership of the account and everything we build in it.

### Do you run Klaviyo SMS as well as email?

Yes. We set up Klaviyo SMS with compliant consent capture, quiet hours and separate flows for subscribers who opt in to texts. SMS suits time-sensitive messages like shipping updates, launches and back in stock alerts, so we plan it together with email and avoid sending the same message on both channels.

### How do you measure Klaviyo results?

We measure flow revenue, campaign revenue, revenue per recipient, list growth and deliverability, checked against your store and GA4 data. Reports come monthly alongside your other channels, so email and SMS are judged on revenue rather than on open rates, which Apple Mail Privacy Protection now inflates.

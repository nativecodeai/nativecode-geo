Questions

## Topic clusters questions

### What exactly is a topic cluster?

A topic cluster is a pillar page and the articles that support it. One pillar page plus its supporting articles, fully interlinked, roughly 15,000 engineered words per cluster. Every page ships with JSON-LD schema, entity optimization and answer blocks, published to your site and validated.

### Is this content writing or something more?

More. We deliver engineered clusters, not raw word counts. Every cluster is built to rank in Google and get cited in AI answers, so research, structure, schema, internal links and publishing are part of the work. Writing is one step in that process, not the whole service.

### How does the process work?

You pick a volume and share the queries you want to own. We map the entities, gaps and search intent behind each one, then write, mark up, link and publish the full cluster. You review drafts before they go live, and we validate every page after publishing.

### Do clusters target AI answer engines?

Yes. Every cluster targets citation in AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity alongside Google rankings. The method holds a verified unbranded AI Overview citation on a DA 34 domain. Answer blocks, clear definitions and consistent entities make each page easier for those tools to quote.

### How do you measure success?

We measure rankings, AI citations and demand captured. Larger programs include AI Overview citation tracking and quarterly reviews tied to revenue. Each cluster is reported as a unit, so you can see how the pillar and its supporting pages perform together.

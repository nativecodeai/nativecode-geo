The search problem

## Why does every location need its own SEO plan?

Every location needs its own plan because each one is its own search market. Map results depend on distance from the searcher, so a branch only ranks well near its own address, with its own profile, page and reviews.

01

### Profiles drift

Hours, categories and phone numbers change one location at a time, and the errors spread to maps and directories.

02

### Copied location pages

Pages that only swap the city name rarely rank and can be treated as doorway pages.

03

### No view across locations

Without reporting by location, it is hard to see which ones lead and which are falling behind.

FAQ

## Multi-location SEO questions

### Does every location need its own page?

Yes, if it has its own address. Each location page should be linked from a crawlable store locator, link to its Google Business Profile and carry details that are unique to that location, such as hours, team, services offered there and nearby landmarks. Thin copies of one template rarely rank.

### How do we avoid duplicate location pages?

Use one template for structure, then add what only that location has. That means its team, the services offered there, nearby landmarks, local reviews and real photos. Pages that differ only by city name can be treated as duplicates, so the unique details are what make each page worth indexing.

### Can franchisees manage their own profiles?

They can, but brand-level control of the core data prevents errors. We usually keep the brand as the owner of every Google Business Profile and give each location manager access to posts, photos and review replies. That keeps names, categories and hours consistent while local teams stay involved.

### How do you report across many locations?

Each location is tracked for calls, direction requests, leads and map rankings, then ranked side by side. You can see which locations lead, which need work and why. Reports roll up to the brand level each month, with notes on what changed and what we will do next.

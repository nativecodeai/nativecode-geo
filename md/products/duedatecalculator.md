# DueDateCalculator.ai Due Date Calculator

Overview

## What DueDateCalculator.ai is

DueDateCalculator.ai takes one known date and returns the whole pregnancy timeline. It supports the three dating methods clinicians actually use (last menstrual period, conception date, and IVF embryo transfer with the embryo's age), and every one of them lands on the same forty-week horizon.

The site is private by architecture: every calculation runs in the browser and no date is sent or stored. That matters in reproductive health, where several popular apps have faced regulatory action over data sharing.

Six calculators share one dating engine, so a conception date, a reverse due date, and a gestational-age reading never disagree with each other. Ten plain-language definitions and a definition-first FAQ are written to be quoted by answer engines, and a first-trimester ultrasound is always named as the authority that sets the official date.

Method

## The math, stated plainly

Every NativeCode product publishes its assumptions on the page. This is the sequence the engine follows.

1. 01

    ### From the last period

    `EDD = LMP + 280 days`

    The standard convention. It assumes a 28-day cycle with ovulation near day 14; a different cycle length shifts the estimate by the difference from 28.

2. 02

    ### From conception

    `EDD = conception + 266 days`

    Two weeks fewer than the LMP count, which is why 40 weeks from the last period is roughly 38 weeks of development.

3. 03

    ### From IVF transfer

    `EDD = transfer + 266 − embryo age`

    A day-5 blastocyst is transfer + 261 days. Because the embryo's age is known exactly, this is the most precise self-calculation available.

4. 04

    ### Confirmed by ultrasound

    A first-trimester crown-rump measurement is the most accurate dating method. When it disagrees significantly with LMP dating, clinicians rely on the ultrasound, and only a clinician sets the official date.

## Questions about DueDateCalculator.ai

Which dating methods does it support?

Three: last menstrual period with an adjustable cycle length, conception date, and IVF embryo transfer with a day-3, day-5 or day-6 embryo age. Each method shows only the inputs it needs, and all three land on the same forty-week horizon, with a first-trimester ultrasound named as the authority.

Does it store my dates?

No. Calculations run in the browser on your device, and nothing is transmitted or stored, so there is no server to leak from. That matters in reproductive health, where several popular apps have faced regulatory action over data sharing. Nothing you enter leaves the browser.

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a strong center point, not a guarantee. Only about 1 in 20 babies arrive on the estimated due date. A first-trimester ultrasound is the most accurate dating method, and when it disagrees significantly with last-period dating, clinicians rely on the ultrasound.

# SizeGuide.ai: AI-Powered Apparel Size Guide

Overview

## What SizeGuide.ai is

SizeGuide.ai extends the Brasize.ai approach (a precise, honest, method-first fit calculator) to apparel more broadly. It exists to answer the question every online shopper has at checkout: will this fit? The goal is fewer returns and more confidence at the moment of purchase, for shoppers and for the stores that serve them.

The approach is measurement-first: sizes vary by brand, bodies don’t. A small set of body measurements anchors every recommendation, mapped against published brand charts, with the reasoning shown rather than hidden.

It is the newest product in the family, and it ships the NativeCode way: a stated method, a published editorial standard, and pages structured for answer engines. It shares its fit engine and design system with Brasize.ai as the toolset expands.

## Questions about SizeGuide.ai

What does SizeGuide.ai do?

It translates body measurements into recommended apparel sizes per garment type and per brand, converts sizes across US, UK, EU and international letter sizes, and diagnoses fit problems such as tight shoulders or long sleeves. Each recommendation shows which measurement drove it, so a between-sizes result is explainable.

How does it relate to Brasize.ai?

It is the second product in the NativeCode fit and sizing family. SizeGuide.ai extends the Brasize.ai approach to apparel more broadly and shares its fit engine, converter pattern, editorial standard and design system. As the newest product in the family, its toolset and reference pages are still expanding.

Which sizing systems does it cover?

SizeGuide.ai covers US, UK and EU numeric apparel sizes plus international letter sizes, so a US 8 reads as a UK 12, an EU 40 and an M. Brand-by-brand size charts and comparisons sit alongside the converter, and more brand charts are added as the reference grows.

Is it free?

Yes. Like every NativeCode instrument, the core SizeGuide.ai tools are free with no sign-up. Shoppers can get a recommended size, convert between regions, check brand size charts and read fit guidance by garment type without needing to create an account first.

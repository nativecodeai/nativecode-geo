# Search architecture for an online retailer with a large catalog.

NativeCode, an Atlanta-based search agency, rebuilt site structure, structured data and search-led product work for an online retailer with a large, fast-changing catalog, turning organic search into a durable growth channel alongside the client's product, engineering and merchandising teams.

## What was holding the catalog back?

HauteFlair is an online retailer with a large, fast-changing catalog. At that size, small structural problems compound: wasted crawl budget, duplicate category pages and templates that slow down discovery of new products.

02

## What changed in the site architecture?

We rebuilt the taxonomy, internal linking and URL rules so search engines spend their time on the pages that earn revenue, and expanded the set of high-intent category and content pages the site could rank with.

## Structured data and performance

Schema.org markup, Core Web Vitals work and platform fixes improved index coverage and how products appear in search results, giving every product page a clear, machine-readable identity.

04

## Organic growth and conversion

The work grew from technical SEO into a full organic program, run alongside the client's product, engineering, UX and merchandising teams. It included a product page rebuild that raised conversion, and EDI connections to suppliers that keep inventory and order routing in sync with the catalog.

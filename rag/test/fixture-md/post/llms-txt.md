# llms.txt: what it is and whether you need one

An llms.txt file is a Markdown index at the root of a domain that tells language models which pages matter and what each one covers. Adoption is early and no engine requires it.

## Should you publish one?

Publishing one costs little. Keep it under 100 KB, link your most important pages with one-line descriptions, and update it when content ships.

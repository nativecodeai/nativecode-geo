# Shopify development

We design and build Shopify and Shopify Plus stores for growing brands. Every build ships with structured data, clean collection architecture and fast templates.

## What is included

- Custom theme development on Online Store 2.0
- Migrations from Magento, WooCommerce and BigCommerce with redirect mapping
- App integrations for subscriptions, reviews and loyalty

## How long does a Shopify build take?

Most stores launch in eight to twelve weeks, depending on catalog size and integrations.

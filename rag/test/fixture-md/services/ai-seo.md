# AI SEO

AI search optimization gets your brand named in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. We structure pages so answer engines can quote them, add schema that resolves your entity, and track citations over time.

## Answer engine optimization

Answer engine optimization (AEO) means writing question-led sections with a direct answer of forty to sixty words, followed by supporting detail. Engines lift those blocks verbatim.

## Generative engine optimization

Generative engine optimization (GEO) covers crawler access, entity reconciliation with sameAs links, and citable content blocks that large language models can reuse.
